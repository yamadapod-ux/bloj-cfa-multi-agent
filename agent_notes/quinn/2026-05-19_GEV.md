### Kelly Criterion [CFA L3: Portfolio Management — Kelly Criterion]

Kelly Formula: K = (p × b - q) / b
- p = probability of win (FV > ราคา): **20%** (เฉพาะ cells ≥ $1,050)
- q = probability of loss: **80%**
- b = win/loss ratio: avg upside case (+15%) / avg downside case (-35%) ≈ 0.43x

K = (0.20 × 0.43 - 0.80) / 0.43 = **NEGATIVE**

**Kelly = Negative ณ ราคา $1,050** → ไม่ควร open new position
