Methodology:
- Sector: Industrials high-growth — typical beta range 1.2–1.8
- Comparable: POWL beta ~1.4 (60% growth industrial), ETN beta ~1.1 (mature)
- FPS growth rate (103% YoY) and leverage (Net Debt/EBITDA 3.73x) = high-beta adjustment
- 52W price range: $25.95–$51.00 = 97% range = high realized volatility
- Estimated Beta: **1.60** (conservative vs potential 2.0+ given extreme growth + leverage)

Note: Beta ไม่สามารถคำนวณจาก regression ได้เนื่องจากไม่มี historical daily price series — estimate จาก comparable

**WACC Calculation:**
