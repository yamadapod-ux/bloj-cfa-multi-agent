## 5. Additional Quant Metrics

### Hypothesis Testing — Outperformance [CFA L1: Hypothesis Testing]
- H₀: FICO generates no excess return vs S&P 500
- H₁: FICO outperforms S&P 500 (one-tailed)
- Test: 5Y alpha +6.8%/yr, t-stat = 1.75
- Critical value (90% confidence, one-tail): t = 1.645
- **Result: REJECT H₀ at 90% confidence (t=1.75 > 1.645)** — but only marginal; fail to reject at 95%
- Conclusion: Marginal evidence of genuine outperformance — consistent with FICO's historical pricing power, BUT recent 1Y period shows significant alpha destruction from regulatory event
