## 6. Kelly Criterion & Position Sizing [CFA L3: Kelly Criterion — Optimal Position Size]

### Kelly Formula
- Win probability (p): 76% (based on sensitivity matrix — 19/25 scenarios show positive return)
- Win magnitude (W): +40% (base case FV $567 from $445)
- Loss probability (q): 24%
- Loss magnitude (L): -25% (bear case floor ~$335 from VaR analysis)
