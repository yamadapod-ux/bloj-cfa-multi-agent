| Revenue scenario | Rev | Op margin 20% | Op margin 25% | Op margin 30% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **FY2024-style −23.4%** | $10,634M | EPS ~$3.82 → P/E 95x | ~$4.77 → 76x | ~$5.73 → 63x |

**The forward-P/E 22.6x is entirely dependent on the EPS inflection.** Market forward EPS ~$16 implies TTM GAAP EPS ($8.42) nearly **doubling in ~2 years** (fwd EPS ~1.9× TTM). If EPS instead reaches:
- **$12** (still +43% off TTM, a strong outcome) → forward P/E = **30.1x**

So the "cheap-looking" 22.6x forward multiple has ~35–60% downside embedded if the earnings inflection disappoints even moderately — and that's *before* any multiple compression from the Fed. The GAAP↔adjusted gap ($8.42 GAAP vs ~$16 forward, part of which is the intangible-amort roll-off narrative) makes the "forward EPS" figure less clean than it looks.

**Quantified peak-earnings downside:** a FY2024-style revenue reversion with margins compressing to 25% (still well above the FY2024 trough) → GAAP EPS ~$4.77 → at a de-rated 25x → **~$119/share (−67%)**. ## 9. Kelly Criterion [CFA L3: Portfolio Management — Optimal Position Sizing *(ยังไม่เรียนในหลักสูตรมาตรฐาน — Kelly ใช้ probability/odds กำหนดสัดส่วน bet ที่ maximize long-run log growth)*]

From §6 scenarios (return vs $361.78): Bull +15% (p=0.25) is the only positive outcome; Base −27.5% and Bear −64.5% are losses.
```
P(win) = 0.25   P(loss) = 0.75
Avg win  = +15.0%
Avg loss (prob-weighted) = [0.50(27.5%) + 0.25(64.5%)] / 0.75 = 39.8%
Odds b = 15.0% / 39.8% = 0.377
Kelly f* = (p·b − q) / b = (0.25·0.377 − 0.75) / 0.377 = −174%
Full Kelly ≈ −174%   |   Half ≈ −87%   |   Quarter ≈ −44%
```
**→ Kelly deeply negative at every fraction. No long position is mathematically justified at $361.78.** Same result as MU (−28% full) and HIMS/TMDX (0%). The negative sign says the risk/reward is inverted — the market price is on the wrong side of the probability-weighted distribution.
