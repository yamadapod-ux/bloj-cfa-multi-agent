## 5. Kelly Criterion + Position Sizing [CFA L3: Kelly Criterion]

### Kelly Calculation
- Win probability (p) = 56% (scenarios ≥ fair value, from sensitivity matrix)
- Win multiple: avg FV in positive scenarios = $310 vs $229 = +35.4% gain
- Loss probability (q) = 44%
- Loss amount: avg FV in negative scenarios = $190 vs $229 = -17.0% loss

**Kelly fraction = p/loss - q/win = 0.56/0.170 - 0.44/0.354 = 3.29 - 1.24 = 2.05**

**Full Kelly = 205% (massively concentrated — theoretical only)**
**Half-Kelly = 103% (still high — suggesting very favorable odds)**
