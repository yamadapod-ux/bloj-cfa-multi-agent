## 5. Kelly Criterion [CFA L3: Position Sizing — Kelly]

**Inputs (probability-weighted):**
- P(win) ≈ 65% (Bull + Base scenarios)
- W (avg gain) = +35%
- L (avg loss) = -15%

**Kelly % = (p×W – q×L) / (W×L expression simplified)**
- Kelly = (0.65 × 0.35 − 0.35 × 0.15) / (0.35 × 0.15) approach simplified to f* ≈ 0.65 − (0.35/2.33) = **~50% raw**
- Half-Kelly applied (institutional) → ~25% raw
- Capped to portfolio risk budget → **5–7% position recommended**
