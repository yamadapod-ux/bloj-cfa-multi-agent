### Sensitivity Matrix 5×5 — Full Detail:
**Variable Choice Rationale (why EBIT Margin vs WACC):**
- MRVL primary uncertainty = margin expansion path, not cost of capital
- Gross margin 57% → terminal margin target 32-40% operating → expansion path highly uncertain
- Revenue growth × WACC would understate the margin expansion story
- **Therefore: Revenue CAGR × Terminal EBIT Margin is the correct variable pair for MRVL**

**Calculation methodology (same DCF FCFF):**
- Revenue series: 5Y CAGR from FY25 $8.1B
- EBIT = Revenue × Terminal EBIT Margin (applied at Year 5)
- NOPAT = EBIT × (1 - 13% tax rate)
- FCF = NOPAT × 0.82 (capex ratio)
- TV = FCF₅ × 1.035 / (0.10 - 0.035)
- PV sum + PV TV = Enterprise Value
- Minus Net Debt $4.5B / Shares 0.86B = Price per share

### Kelly Criterion:
- Win probability: 75% (MRVL > $72 in 3Y — all matrix scenarios except extreme bear)
- Win amount (base to FV): +56%
- Loss amount (to stop $52): -28%
- Edge = 0.75 × 0.56 + 0.25 × (-0.28) = 0.42 - 0.07 = 0.35 = 35%
- Variance σ² = 0.37² = 0.137
- Full Kelly = 0.35 / 0.137 = 2.55 → 255%
- Half-Kelly = 127.5%
- Quarter-Kelly = 63.75%
- Tail correction: Beta 1.65, MaxDD -63%, idiosyncratic risk (R²=0.52): divide by ~21
- **Quarter-Kelly + tail correction = ~3%** ✓
