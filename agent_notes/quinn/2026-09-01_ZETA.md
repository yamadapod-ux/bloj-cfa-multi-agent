## 2. DCF — NOPAT-based FCFF  [CFA L2: Equity Valuation — FCFF Model]

### FCFF build (basis disclosure — DCF Cash Flow Consistency Rule)
I start from **GAAP Operating Income $53.4M (TTM, 3.4% margin)** — which **already deducts the $194.5M TTM SBC** as an operating expense. This is the NOPAT/operating-income basis, matching Emma.

```
FCFF = EBIT × (1 − tax) + (D&A − CapEx run-off) − ΔNWC
```
- **SBC treatment:** expensed in full (inside EBIT). I do **NOT** start from reported FCF $224.4M — that number is inflated by adding SBC back into operating cash flow. Reported FCF margin 14.3% ≠ sustainable NOPAT-based FCFF margin (~5–6% today). SBC-adjusted "owner" FCF ≈ $30M (Atlas §D) confirms the true cash economics are thin.
- **Dilution:** consistent with expensing SBC, I hold share count roughly flat (net buyback ≈ offsets SBC issuance) rather than compounding dilution on top — i.e., SBC is charged once, through the P&L. If ZETA stops buying back stock, share count grows ~3–4%/yr and per-share FV falls a further ~5–8%.
- **D&A − CapEx:** +$15M yr 1 fading to $0 by yr 6 (acquired-intangible amortisation, chiefly LiveIntent, rolls off; CapEx is only ~1% of revenue — asset-light).
- **ΔNWC:** −1.5% of incremental revenue (receivables grow with the media pass-through book; partly offset by media payables).
- **Base year:** FY2026E revenue **$1,818M** (raised guide) — projections run FY2027→FY2036.
