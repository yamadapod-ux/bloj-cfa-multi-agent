### Sensitivity Matrix 5×5 — Full Detail:
**Variable 1:** Revenue CAGR (Y1–5) [10%, 20%, 28%, 36%, 45%]
**Variable 2:** WACC [8%, 9%, 10%, 11%, 12%]
**Method:** DCF FCFF, terminal growth = 3.5%, EBIT margin at terminal = 28%

**Calculation methodology:**
1. Project revenue for 5 years using each CAGR
2. Apply terminal EBIT margin to Year 5 revenue → NOPAT
3. FCF = NOPAT × 0.80 (capex adjustment)
4. Discount sum of FCF + Terminal Value (TV = FCF₅ × 1.035 / (WACC - 0.035))
5. Divide by shares outstanding (1.27B)

| **10%** | $95 | $85 | $77 | $70 | $64 |

**Key Insights:**

### Kelly Criterion Calculation:
**Expected Return (p×W + (1-p)×(-L)):**
- Win probability (p): 72% (AMD > $105 in 3Y based on base scenario being >$105 in most of matrix)
- Win amount (W): +41% (base case to FV $148)
- Loss amount (L): -26% (to stop-loss $78)
- Edge = 0.72 × 0.41 + 0.28 × (-0.26) = 0.295 - 0.073 = +0.222 = 22.2%
- Variance (σ²): Return std² = 0.37² = 0.137
- Full Kelly = Edge / Variance = 0.222 / 0.137 = 1.62 → 162% (clearly nonsensical, use fractional)
- Half-Kelly: 81% (still too high)
- Quarter-Kelly: 40.5%
- With tail correction (Beta 1.75, Max DD 57%, non-normal distribution): divide by 10
- **Quarter-Kelly + tail correction: ~4%** ✓
