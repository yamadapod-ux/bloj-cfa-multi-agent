## Key Learning Recorded

### New Framework Elements (V Analysis)

1. **Payment Network WACC Adjustment**: For companies with active federal antitrust litigation, WACC should include regulatory risk premium (+1-1.5%) not captured in market beta. V beta 0.90 does NOT reflect DOJ lawsuit risk.

2. **Toll Road Narrative Fallacy Pattern**: Beautiful business narratives (toll road, picks-and-shovels) are especially susceptible to Narrative Fallacy bias. Bear's challenge #1 should always probe "what is the meta-challenge to this narrative?"

3. **First Financial Services (Payment Network) Analysis**: V is the first pure-play payment network analyzed. Key differences from semiconductors/infrastructure:
   - Much lower beta (0.90) → lower CAPM required return → easier to justify P/E premium
   - BUT: Idiosyncratic regulatory risk (antitrust) NOT in beta → must add explicit risk premium
   - Asset-light model → FCF conversion very high (>95%) → DCF terminal value dominates
   - Network effects moat → most durable moat type in portfolio but also most exposed to government regulatory disruption

4. **Portfolio Diversification**: V would be first Financial Services position. Post-entry: 19% semiconductor + 4% financial services → healthy diversification. Different risk factor than existing holdings.

5. **DOJ Antitrust as Standalone Risk Factor**: Unlike semiconductor export restrictions (AMD/NVDA/MRVL) which affect sector broadly, V's antitrust case is company-specific. Should be modeled as binary event probability in scenario weighting, not just as qualitative risk note.
