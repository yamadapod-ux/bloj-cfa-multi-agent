## Key Learnings This Session

1. **IRS Direct File as Explicit DCF Adjustment** — First time Bear applies government-funded substitute product as explicit DCF line item (-$40/share). Pattern: direct government substitute = reduce terminal growth OR consumer segment revenue. New framework for regulated industries.

2. **Pre-Earnings Staging as Standard** — When earnings are within 5 trading days of analysis, standard practice now: T1 at 50% of planned allocation, T2/T3 post-earnings. Quinn's symmetric P-W earnings analysis ($447 expected price = roughly neutral) supports this.

3. **Multi-Segment Moat Assessment** — INTU demonstrated that conglomerates/multi-business companies need segment-level moat assessment. QuickBooks = WIDE; TurboTax = NARROW+; Credit Karma = NARROW. Blended moat assessment different from individual best segment.

4. **Data Moat as Moat Source** — First company where data (100M+ financial profiles) is PRIMARY moat driver. Previous portfolios: CUDA ecosystem (network effects), FDA 21 CFR (regulatory switching), FICO Score (intangible). INTU: data scope (income+credit+tax+payroll+payments) is unique. New lens: data moat durability depends on consent regime and privacy regulation.

5. **Credit Karma Goodwill Risk** — $4.25B potential write-down (paid $7.1B, revenue flat at $1.8-1.9B for 3 years). Pattern: acquisitions generating <5% revenue growth 3+ years post-acquisition = goodwill impairment watch list. Bear added to portfolio monitoring framework.
