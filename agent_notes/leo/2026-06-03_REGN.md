## Key Learning Points — REGN Session

### New Sector: Healthcare / Pharma-Biotech (First dedicated pharma analysis)

1. **Pharma WACC = 8.0-8.5%** (significantly lower than tech 9.5-11%) due to:
   - Lower beta (0.65-0.75 for large-cap pharma vs 1.2-1.8 for semis)
   - Investment-grade credit (not speculative)
   - Defensive revenue (drug prescriptions don't stop in recession)
   - Pattern: WACC ladder established: Pharma 8-8.5% < Value Tech 9-9.5% < Growth Tech 10-11%

2. **Sensitivity Variable for Pharma: Drug CAGR × Regulatory Risk Probability**
   - REGN: Dupixent Revenue CAGR × IRA Pricing Haircut
   - Different from: Tech (Revenue CAGR × WACC) | Defense (Revenue CAGR × EBITDA Margin) | Semis (Revenue CAGR × China %)
   - Pattern: Variable 2 is always the MOST RELEVANT RISK SPECIFIC to the sector

3. **Pharma-Specific Bear Challenge: IRA Drug Pricing**
   - Inflation Reduction Act = new structural risk for profitable biologics
   - Dupixent Medicare spend > $1B threshold = eligible for CMS negotiation
   - IRA risk is NOT in WACC (binary, not continuous) — must be separate scenario
   - Framework: IRA Probability × Price Cut % × Revenue Impact = Bear scenario weight

4. **Collaboration Revenue = Double-Edged Sword**
   - Sanofi collaboration = REGN can't scale Dupixent alone (50% profit only)
   - BUT Sanofi's global distribution = REGN gets global reach without building sales force
   - Moat assessment: Wide moat partially co-owned = durability concern is legitimate
   - Bear raised correctly: "Wide moat is partly Sanofi's moat"

5. **Patent Cliff = TGR Floor Debate**
   - Emma TGR 2.5% vs Bear TGR 1.0% = $604 DCF gap
   - Resolution: For pharma, TGR post-patent cliff should reflect pipeline replacement risk
   - If pipeline strong (Fianlimab, Linvoseltamab) → 2.0-2.5% justified
   - If pipeline fails → TGR can go negative briefly (earnings revision)
   - Learning: Pharma TGR range 1.0-3.0% (wider than SaaS 2.5-4.0%)
