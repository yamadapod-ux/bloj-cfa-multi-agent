## Key Decisions & Learnings

### New Framework Applied: S&P 500 Inclusion Flow Analysis
- Quinn computed passive flow impact: ~$3.5B mandatory buy (8.4 days avg volume)
- Bear challenged: announcement premium priced in; residual +1-3%
- **Lesson: S&P 500 inclusion = TIMING catalyst, not VALUATION catalyst. Weight accordingly.**
- Bear's position more analytically defensible (event already announced, front-run)

### ROIC Inflation from Excess Cash — Important Analytical Correction
- Emma cited ROIC 115.9% (as reported)
- Bear correctly identified: VEEV's $4.5B excess cash lowers invested capital denominator artificially
- Adjusted ROIC ~15-20% (still > WACC 10%, still good — but not "astounding")
- **Lesson: For cash-rich companies (VEEV, ADBE, etc.) ALWAYS compute both reported and cash-adjusted ROIC. The difference can be dramatic.**
- This is the 2nd time this issue arose (ADBE also has large cash position)

### Vault CRM Migration = Key Catalyst Identified
- VEEV migrating off Salesforce platform (2022 announcement → 2025-2026 execution)
- Migration is IN PROGRESS → customers have a brief window to evaluate alternatives
- **THIS is the single biggest unmodeled risk in consensus analyst models**
- **Lesson: Major platform migrations in SaaS create temporary churn windows. Monitor retention carefully in Q1-Q2 FY2027 data (Jun and Aug 2026).**

### Healthcare SaaS — First Position Analysis
- VEEV = first Healthcare SaaS name in portfolio
- Different from semiconductor names (NVDA/AMD/MRVL/AVBO) and Creative SaaS (ADBE)
- Moat type: REGULATORY switching cost (not CUDA lock-in, not creative workflow lock-in)
- **Lesson: Regulatory SaaS has the most durable moat but also the most binary risk (regulatory change risk). Treat FDA 21 CFR Part 11 modernization as tail risk to monitor.**

### Position Sizing Ladder Update
- VEEV: 3% max (HOLD signal; enter if price pullback or Bull Flip catalyst)
- Position sizing rationale: similar to MRVL (3%) — thin MOS profile + execution risk (Vault CRM migration)
