## Challenge 2: "ROIC 115.9%" is Likely Overstated / Misleading [CFA L1: Financial Reporting — ROIC Quality]

Emma cites ROIC = 115.9%. This number sounds extraordinary. Let me dissect it.
- VEEV has $4.5B net cash on balance sheet
- When you have excess cash, Invested Capital (denominator of ROIC) = low → ROIC inflated
- **Correct ROIC calculation should ADD cash to invested capital if cash is truly idle**
- Alternatively: ROIC = NOPAT / (Total Assets − Non-operating cash − Non-operating liabilities)
- If we add back $4.5B excess cash to denominator: Invested Capital rises from ~$1B to ~$5.5B
- **Adjusted ROIC = ~$850M NOPAT / ~$5.5B Invested Capital = ~15.5%** — still good, but not 115.9%
- WACC = 10%, adjusted ROIC = 15.5% → spread = 5.5% → modest EVA, not spectacular
- **Bear ROIC fair = ~15-20% (adjusted)** → still justifies premium but NOT 115x multiple premium

**Bear's challenge: ROIC 115.9% is an accounting artifact. True economic ROIC ~15-20%.**
