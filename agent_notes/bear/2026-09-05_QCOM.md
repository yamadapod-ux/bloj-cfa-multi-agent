## 4. Challenge: Is the WACC/beta spike a permanent re-rating or transient noise?

**This is the single highest-value challenge in this session — and it's Quinn's own flag, which I'm independently corroborating and quantifying further.**

Beta moved from 1.35 → 1.68 (+24%) in under 4 months, entirely mechanical from Yahoo's trailing 5-year **monthly** regression. A single sharp, concentrated -22.6% drawdown month will mechanically inflate a trailing-beta estimate because that period's covariance with the market spikes during the crash itself — this is a textbook estimation-window artifact [CFA L1: Quantitative Methods — regression sensitivity to outlier/regime-shift observations within the sample window], not necessarily evidence of a structurally higher forward-looking systematic risk.

**Counter-argument (why it might NOT be pure noise):** the whole semiconductor group is trading at elevated systematic sensitivity right now (AVGO, MRVL at extreme multiples = higher beta/vol sector-wide), and QCOM specifically now carries a *persistent* (not one-time) source of idiosyncratic-turned-systematic risk: a live, recurring China-truce renewal cycle (Q2 above) that keeps generating event risk every few months. A beta that stays elevated because the *underlying risk generator* (trade-policy binary risk) is recurring, not because of a one-off crash artifact, would be a legitimate structural re-rating, not noise.

**Sensitivity Test (required per house rule when Bear challenges a number):**
> **If beta mean-reverts from 1.68 back toward its pre-crash 5Y level of ~1.35** (Rf unchanged at 4.78%): Cost of Equity falls from 13.18-13.19% to ~11.53%, and WACC falls from **12.5% → ~11.0%** (-150bps), using the same CAPM/capital-structure formula Emma and Quinn both used. Per **Quinn's own stated sensitivity** ("a 100bp WACC decline alone moves Base FV from ~$92 toward the $130-150 range, holding growth/margin constant"), a ~150bp decline would plausibly move Base-case FV from the current **$92-96 (Emma/Quinn base)** to roughly **$145-175**.
> **Effect on Bear's own P-W EV (below):** would rise from **~$103 to roughly $130-150** — still short of the $194 BUY threshold, but a **meaningful narrowing** of the gap, and enough to potentially bring Blended FV (once Bear/Emma/Quinn are reconciled) into a range where a modest MOS improvement is plausible if beta normalizes over 2-3 quarters.

**Verdict on Q4:** I judge this **partially transient** — likely some mean-reversion is coming (beta rarely stays at a crash-inflated peak for more than a few quarters absent continued realized volatility), but not a full round-trip back to 1.35, because the China-truce recurrence and confirmed Apple erosion are real, ongoing risk generators that justify *some* permanent uplift versus the May 10.75% WACC. My working assumption for Bear's own scenario weighting below: beta partially reverts toward **~1.50-1.55** in the base case over the next 2-3 quarters (not fully to 1.35, not staying at 1.68).
