## Challenges ต่อ Emma's Thesis [CFA L2: Equity — Risk Analysis]

### Challenge 1: DCF Assumptions ทั้งหมดใช้ "Business as Usual" — Apple Transition ไม่ถูก Model อย่างถูกต้อง

Emma คาด 12% FCF CAGR (base) + 18% (bull) โดย **ไม่ discount Apple revenue อย่างชัดเจน**

**Bear's Case:**
- Apple revenue ~$8-10B (ใน $47B total = ~18-21%)
- Timeline: iPhone 2025 (15% Apple modem share) → iPhone 2026 (40-50% Apple modem) → iPhone 2027 (100% Apple modem) — based on Apple supply chain announcements
- Even in Emma's "base": **$8-10B revenue disappears by 2027** = -$1.8B-$2.2B FCF impact
- Emma's FCF base $11B - $2.0B (Apple) = $9B FCF base → DCF FV ลง ~$20-25/share

**Bear's Revised DCF Base:**
- Adjusted FCF base: $9.0B (after Apple modem loss 2027)
- CAGR 10% (growth from Automotive + IoT offset Apple loss)
- TV growth 2.5% (lower due to IP licensing pressure)
- Adjusted DCF FV: ~$165-175

### Challenge 2: China 53% = Undiversifiable Risk — Not "Priced In" ตาม Emma

Emma กล่าวว่า China risk = "discount" ในการให้ P/E 22x แต่ Bear contends:

**Bear's Quantification:**
- China revenue (53% of $49B) = ~$26B/yr
- QCOM gross margin on China sales ~55% → China gross profit ~$14.3B
- If US export restrictions expand to include QCT chips for Chinese OEMs:
  - Revenue impact: -$15-$26B (partial to full)
  - FCF impact: -$3.3B to -$5.7B (at 22% FCF margin)
  - Fair value impact: **-$35 to -$53/share**
- 90-day US-China truce expires July 2026 = **7 weeks from now**
- Probability of escalation (Bear estimate): 30-40% (not 15-20% as Emma implies)

**Bear argues: Emma's "discount P/E" assumption of ~8% discount vs peers is completely insufficient compensation for a 30-40% probability event that could wipe -$35 to -$53/share**

### Challenge 3: Automotive Pipeline $45B = Marketing Number, Not Financial Reality

Emma heavily weights automotive as "long-term upside catalyst"

**Bear's Challenge:**
- $45B pipeline spans 8 years (= $5.6B/yr at full ramp)
- Current automotive revenue: ~$2.9B/yr (only $1.9B annualized uplift from pipeline)
- Win rate on auto pipelines historically: 60-70% (not all wins = revenue)
- Timeline to full ramp: 5-8 years = heavily discounted in DCF
- Terminal value is where automotive benefit sits = **Emma may be over-weighting near-term automotive in Stage 1**
- EV market growth has slowed significantly in 2025-2026 (Consumer pullback, government subsidy cuts)

**Revised automotive contribution:** $3.5B by FY2027 (not $5B+ as bull case implies) = less Stage 1 uplift

### Challenge 4: Arm Architecture Competitive Threat More Serious Than Emma Suggests

Emma rates Arm threat as "moderate" under switching costs

**Bear's Case:**
- Arm's IPO 2023 + SoftBank control = now direct competitor strategy
- MediaTek Dimensity 9400 (flagship mobile chip) closing performance gap with Snapdragon 8 Elite
- Samsung re-investing in Exynos with Arm IP
- Google Tensor G4 (Arm custom) = another de-Qualcomm-ization example
- **Bear thesis: Snapdragon's market share in Android flagship could decline from ~75% to 60-65% by 2028** as Arm custom chips gain ground
- Each 5% market share loss in flagship Android = -$1.5B revenue = -$0.33B FCF = -$3/share FV impact

### Challenge 5: QTL Licensing — "Endless" Revenue Stream at Risk

Emma treats QTL as ~$4.8B pure-profit moat. Bear challenges:

**Bear's Evidence:**
- FTC vs QCOM (2019): District court found QCOM violated antitrust — reversed on appeal, but FRAND pressure continues
- European Commission fine €997M (2018)
- Korea KFTC fine $865M (2016)
- China NDRC fine $975M (2015)
- **Pattern:** Every major jurisdiction has investigated or fined QCOM for licensing practices
- Probability of FRAND-forced rate reduction in next 5 years: 40%
- If QTL fees reduced 15-20% → QTL revenue $4.8B × 80% = $3.84B → -$0.96B FCF
- FV impact: ~-$9/share

## What Would Change Our Mind [CFA L3: Portfolio Construction — Investment Thesis]

### Bull Flip Triggers (Bear ยอมรับ BUY ถ้าเกิดสิ่งเหล่านี้)
1. **Apple confirms QCOM modem extension ถึง 2028** — eliminates #1 revenue risk; removes -$20-25/share DCF haircut
2. **US-China 90-day truce extends to 12+ months** with QCOM explicitly exempted from semiconductor restrictions — China revenue secure; removes -$35-53/share binary risk
3. **Automotive revenue crosses $4B/yr** (FY2026) — proves pipeline conversion rate ahead of schedule
4. **QTL regulatory settlement reached** (consent decree with clear long-term pricing framework) — removes ongoing litigation overhang
5. **Price corrects to $185-$195** — Bear P-W EV $167 provides insufficient MOS but at $185-190 the bull case upside (+45%) starts to compensate for Bear tail risks

### Bear Flip Triggers (ถ้าเกิดสิ่งนี้ Bear ยืนยัน SELL/AVOID)
1. **Apple confirms 100% in-house modem for iPhone 2026** — $8-10B revenue certainty gone
2. **US export restrictions formally expand to QCT chips** for Chinese OEMs — -$15-26B revenue at risk triggered
3. **QTL court ruling forces FRAND rate reduction >20%** — structural permanent margin compression
4. **MediaTek claims >40% flagship Android market share** (from current ~25%) — QCOM moat cracking quantifiably
5. **Revenue growth ≤ 5% for 2 consecutive quarters** with no China/Apple explanation — organic growth story fails

### Thesis Invalidation (ถ้าเกิดสิ่งนี้ = thesis ผิดทั้งหมด — ต้อง re-analysis ทันที)
- **BOTH Apple transition AND China restriction happen simultaneously** — Combined -$18B revenue = FCF falls to $6-7B = FV ~$85-100 = AVOID immediately
- China revenue drops below 30% involuntarily (not by replacement but by ban) in any single quarter
