## 🚨 Challenge 1: Emma's ROIC 49.15% — Is It Real or Inflated?

**Bear Challenge:** ROIC 49.15% ดู exceptional มาก แต่ต้องตรวจสอบ methodology

**Issues:**
1. **ADSK มี accumulated goodwill สูงจากการ acquisition** (Autodesk ซื้อ PlanGrid $875M 2019, BuildingConnected $275M 2018, Assemble Systems 2018, etc.) → goodwill อยู่ใน invested capital denominator → ถ้า goodwill สูง = IC สูง = ROIC ต่ำลง แต่ถ้า goodwill ถูก amortize หรือ written down มาก = IC ต่ำ = ROIC สูงเกินจริง
2. **Negative Working Capital SaaS pattern** — SaaS companies มักมี deferred revenue สูง (ลูกค้าจ่ายล่วงหน้า) → working capital negative → IC ต่ำ → ROIC สูง โดยไม่สะท้อน "real" economic return เสมอ
3. **ROIC 49.15% vs ADBE ~35-40%** — ADBE ที่มี moat ใกล้เคียงกัน ROIC ต่ำกว่า → ADSK 49.15% อาจ distorted หรือ ADSK genuinely more capital-light?

**Bear conclusion:** ROIC อาจ 35-45% เมื่อ adjust (ยังดีมาก แต่ไม่ใช่ 49.15%); ไม่ invalidate thesis แต่ Emma ควร adjust ใน quality assessment

## 🚨 Challenge 3: AEC Cyclicality — Emma Underestimates Macro Sensitivity

**Bear Challenge:** Emma calls ADSK "low-medium" cyclical — Bear disagrees

**AEC Sector Economics:**
1. **Construction activity is highly cyclical** — US housing starts down ~35% from 2022 peak; commercial real estate (office, retail) still stressed
2. **ADSK revenue lag construction activity by 12-24 months** — firms don't cancel subscriptions immediately (switching cost protects), but they don't expand seats either → ARR growth slows
3. **AEC (~50% of ADSK revenue) with construction slowdown** — if new construction projects pause, architectural firms hire less, buy fewer seats
4. **Historical evidence:** ADSK revenue growth was ~8-10% during 2015-2019 (non-boom period) vs 15-18% now → if construction normalizes, ADSK reverts to 10-12% growth range

**Bear's downside scenario if AEC stays flat 2026-2027:**
- FY27 Revenue growth: 12% (not 18%)
- FY28-29: 10% (not 15%)
- Bear Base FV: ~$205-220

**This is Bear's biggest fundamental concern** — not AI disruption (which is 3-5 year) but near-term AEC cyclicality.

## 🚨 Challenge 6: Behavioral Finance — Recency Bias + Narrative Fallacy [CFA L3: Behavioral Finance]

**Bear Challenge:** The ADSK thesis sounds like ADBE 2022 — and it may be correct but Bear must flag:

1. **Recency Bias** — Q1 FY27 +18% is one quarter after subscription transition; team extrapolating one good quarter forward
2. **Narrative Fallacy** — "ADSK = next ADBE" is a compelling story but:
   - ADBE's subscription transition happened earlier (2013-2017) and was more dramatic (from $10/yr license to $54/mo)
   - ADSK transition was slower and customer base (AEC firms) is less tech-savvy than creative professionals
   - ADBE stock rose 800% post-transition over 5 years; ADSK may not replicate exactly
3. **Anchoring to ATH** — $329 ATH used as "target" framing; but ATH was during 2021 rate-zero bubble period which INFLATED all SaaS
4. **Confirmation Bias** — analyst avg PT $319.27 cited as evidence; but analyst consensus typically mirrors recent momentum
