## 1. Challenging Emma's Equity Analysis

### Challenge 1.1 — DCF Assumption: Revenue CAGR 25% (BASE)

**Emma assumes:** Revenue CAGR 25% for FY2026-2028

**Bear challenges:**
- AMD Q1 2026 revenue ~$7.4B (+38% YoY) — impressive, but driven largely by Data Center MI300X ramp
- Q2/Q3 2026 comps become HARDER — AMD had $5.4B Q1 2025, but will have $7.4B Q1 2026 to comp against by Q1 2027
- NVDA B200 (Blackwell) already in market → AMD MI300X losing NEW design wins to B200 at hyperscalers who prefer CUDA
- AMD MI350 not shipping in volume until H2 2026 at earliest — GAP RISK in H1 2026
- **Bear Revenue CAGR Assessment: 18-20% is more realistic for FY2026-2028; 25% requires bull scenario execution**

**Verdict:** Emma's base CAGR 25% is OPTIMISTIC. Bear would use 18% as base, 25% as bull.

### Challenge 1.2 — Operating Margin Expansion (20%→25%)

**Emma assumes:** Operating margin expands from 18% → 25% by FY2028

**Bear challenges:**
- AMD R&D spending is ~ACCELERATING not decelerating — AMD must invest heavily in MI350, MI400, Zen 6 roadmap to stay competitive vs NVDA/Intel
- R&D as % of revenue: AMD ~15-17% vs NVDA ~10% — AMD less efficient per R&D dollar
- Hiring costs for AI chip engineers are at RECORD LEVELS (NVDA, Google, Meta all competing for same talent pool)
- COGS are rising: CoWoS advanced packaging from TSMC = more expensive per chip
- **Bear Operating Margin: 17-20% realistic cap for FY2028; Emma's 25% requires scale that AMD may not achieve without multiple compression from hyperscaler pushback on pricing**

**Verdict:** Operating margin expansion is partially correct but speed is overstated.

### Challenge 1.3 — ROIC vs WACC Disconnect

**Emma acknowledges** ROIC 5-7% < WACC 10.5% but doesn't fully penalize the valuation.

**Bear challenges:**
- Any company earning ROIC < WACC is DESTROYING economic value
- AMD has been destroying economic value for 3+ years despite revenue growth
- FV should incorporate ECONOMIC PROFIT framework: EP = (ROIC - WACC) × Invested Capital
- AMD Invested Capital ~$35-40B (ex cash) × (6% - 10.5%) = **-$1.6B to -$1.8B/year economic loss**
- To justify $735B market cap on negative EP, you need massive future profitability improvement
- **Bear ROIC requirement:** AMD needs ROIC ≥ WACC (10.5%) by FY2028 minimum; current trajectory has ROIC ~8-9% by FY2028 = STILL below WACC

**Verdict:** Emma underweights ROIC-WACC gap. This is the single biggest bear argument for AMD valuation.

### Challenge 1.4 — Moat Rating NARROW vs Challenger Ceiling

**Emma rates:** NARROW (increasing)

**Bear challenges:**
- EPYC CPU: Yes, gaining share from Intel — but this is because INTEL IS WEAK, not because AMD is exceptional. Intel 18A process is in recovery mode (IFS 2025-2026). Once Intel Panther Lake/Clearwater Forest ship with improved IPC, AMD EPYC premium erodes.
- MI300X AI GPU: Software ecosystem = ROCm is STILL only 15-20% of CUDA's tooling breadth. Hyperscalers use MI300X mostly for inference (less ROCm-dependent) and for diversification, NOT because ROCm is comparable.
- **Ceiling Problem:** AMD cannot become "Wide Moat" while NVDA CUDA remains the developer standard. AMD moat is bounded by NVDA ceiling.
- **NARROW+ is ceiling, not trajectory** — Emma's "increasing" language is too optimistic

**Verdict:** NARROW moat, ceiling at NARROW+. No path to WIDE in 5-year horizon without CUDA displacement.

### Challenge 1.5 — China Revenue 20-25%: Bear Quantification

**Emma flags** China risk but does NOT model it as a discrete variable.

**Bear quantifies:**
- AMD China revenue ~20-25% of total ~$25B = ~$5-6B/yr
- US-China trade truce expires JULY 2026 (~7 weeks from analysis date)
- BIS already restricting H100-level AI chips to China; AMD MI300X may be next
- If AMD China revenue falls 50% (export control + Huawei competition): -$2.5-3.0B revenue loss
- Impact on FV: -$15 to -20 per share on DCF (∼ 10-14% FV reduction)
- **Bear China Scenario:** 35% probability of significant export control tightening by Q4 2026

**Verdict:** China risk should be modeled as discrete binary, NOT just flagged. Emma's FV should include China-adjusted downside: ~$125-128/share (Bear ESG+China adj.)

**Emma rates:** E 6.5/10, S 5.5/10, G 6.5/10 → Overall 6.2/10
