## 1. Challenge to Emma's Thesis

### Challenge 1: Neutron Probability is Optimistic
- Emma assumes 55% success P for maiden launch
- **Bear:** Historical first-launch success rate for new orbital rockets:
  - Falcon 1: failed 3/4 → success only on 4th
  - Astra: ~30% success rate, eventually delisted
  - Virgin Orbit: bankrupt
  - Relativity Terran 1: failed maiden
  - Firefly Alpha: failed maiden, 50% success since
  - **Industry baseline = 35–45% maiden success, 55% only after iteration**
- Plus Neutron is **reusable** medium-lift = harder than expendable Electron
- **Bear's Neutron success P = 40–45%**, not 55%
- **Probability-weighted impact:** -10% to fair value → ~$21 base

### Challenge 2: Neutron Timeline Will Slip Again — Pattern is Clear
- Original plan: 2024 maiden
- 2023 update: 2024 H2 → 2025 H1
- 2024 update: 2025 mid → 2025 H2
- 2025 update: 2026 H1 → 2026 H2
- **Pattern: ~12–18 month average slip per announcement**
- Stage 2 reusability tested 2027+ best case
- **Bear base case: maiden 2027 H1**, full cadence 6/yr by FY30 not FY29
- This is +1yr to revenue ramp = ~-15% DCF

### Challenge 3: Dilution Risk Substantially Higher Than Modeled
- Cash $520M, FCF burn $140M/yr
- Neutron capex remaining ~$200M
- Stage 2 + reusability dev: another $100M
- **At base burn → cash runway 24 months but ZERO buffer**
- Single Neutron launch failure → $80–100M loss event + lost revenue → equity raise mandatory
- History: 2 raises since IPO (2022 PIPE, 2024 ATM, 2025 converts)
- **Dilution scenarios:**
  - Mild (10% increase in shares at $18): -8% per share
  - Moderate (15% at $14): -13% per share
  - Severe (20% at $10): -16% per share
- Emma's bear case "+15% dilution" → I'd say 50% probability for 15–20% over 3yr

### Challenge 4: Customer Concentration & Backlog Quality
- Backlog $1.4B ≠ revenue
- Top 5 customers ~55% of backlog (DoD/NRO heavy)
- Backlog includes "options" (not firm) + customer-funded development
- **Effective firm backlog likely $900M–$1B**
- One customer slip (e.g., Globalstar) = -5% to FY27 revenue
- HASTE program cadence depends on DoD pull, not RKLB push

### Challenge 5: SOTP for Neutron is Circular
- Emma's SOTP gives Neutron $14B success / $1.5B failure × 55/45 = $7.7B blended
- **Problem:** $14B success uses DCF where success probability = 100%, then weights
- Should be: success DCF is ~$11B (already conservative growth), $11×0.55 + $1.5×0.45 = $6.7B
- Plus Neutron failure = company impaired entirely → likely $0 + cash, not $1.5B
- **Bear SOTP: $6.7B + $3.6B (rest) = $10.3B equity → $20.40/share**

### Challenge 6: Peer Premium Not Justified
- RKLB at 16.5x EV/Sales vs peer median 3x is "growth premium"
- BUT growth premium = future cash flow not yet earned
- AVOID compare to ASTS (true binary lottery — biased benchmark)
- Defense peers (LHX, BA-Defense) at 1–2.5x EV/Sales are right comparator post-Neutron-success
- **If Neutron works → 3-5yr re-rate to defense multiple ~6x = $35**
- **If Neutron fails → re-rate to PL/MAXR multiple ~3x = $11**
- Asymmetry not as good as it looks because failure path dominant tail
