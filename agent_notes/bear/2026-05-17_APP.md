## 1. CHALLENGE: EMMA'S FV $410 IS TOO HIGH

**Emma's FV:** $410 (blended DCF 40% + EV/Rev 30% + EV/EBITDA 30%)

**Bear's Counter:**

**Challenge 1A — DCF Base Case Too Optimistic:**
Emma projects Revenue CAGR 22% (base) for 2027-2030. But:
- Q1 2026 revenue $1.84B implies annualized ~$7.36B
- To reach +22% CAGR from $7.4B base → FY2027: $9.0B, FY2028: $11.0B, FY2029: $13.4B
- Mobile gaming ad market total = ~$120B globally. APP currently ~38% share of mobile gaming DSP
- Mathematical ceiling: APP cannot take >70-75% share → at $120B market and 25% take rate, max addressable = $30B software revenue
- At $13.4B FY2029 Software revenue, APP would need ~45% of the TOTAL mobile gaming market (including ad buying direct, not just DSP)
- **Bear assessment: 22% CAGR for 4 years requires e-Commerce DSP to succeed materially — this is NOT yet proven**

**Challenge 1B — Terminal Growth Assumption 3.5%:**
- Emma uses 3.5% terminal growth — implies APP grows at GDP+1% forever after 2031
- For a market that could saturate at ~$30B max addressable (gaming DSP), this is generous
- Bear uses 2.5% terminal growth

**Challenge 1C — EV/Revenue Multiple 22x:**
- Emma applies 22x EV/Revenue to FY2026E $8.2B
- TTD (Trade Desk) = 12x EV/Revenue; META = 8x
- APP gets 22x premium for being fastest-growing — BUT this assumes growth SUSTAINS
- If revenue growth decelerates from 59% to 35% by Q2 2026, multiple compresses to 15-18x immediately
- At 15x EV/Revenue: FV = $8.2B × 15x = $123B / 336M = **$366**
- At 12x EV/Revenue (TTD parity if growth convergges): FV = **$293**

**Bear's DCF (Conservative Assumptions):**
| Scenario Parameter | Bear Value |
|-------------------|-----------|
| Revenue 2026E | $7.8B (vs Emma $8.2B) |
| Rev CAGR 2027-2029 | 25% → 15% (deceleration) |
| Rev CAGR 2030+ | 8% |
| Terminal growth | 2.5% |
| EBITDA Margin | 50% (vs Emma 55%) |
| WACC | 12% (vs Emma 11%) |

Bear DCF FV: **~$215**

**Bear P-W EV Calculation:**
- Bear Scenario (40% prob in Bear's model): $215

**Bear FV: $334**

## 5. MOAT CHALLENGE

**Emma's Moat: NARROW+ (trending toward WIDE)**

**Bear's Challenge:**

**Challenge 5A: Data is NOT as proprietary as Emma claims**
- AppLovin's bid-level data = stored on cloud infrastructure (AWS/GCP)
- Google and Meta have MORE data from their own platforms (own the OS layer via Android, own social graph)
- AXON's training data comes from publisher SDK data — NOT from first-party user accounts
- Limitation: APP cannot do cross-app user matching (ATT blocks this on iOS)
- **Bear rates AXON moat as MEDIUM (not HIGH as Emma implies)**

**Challenge 5B: Unity is NOT the only competitor — Moloco is dangerous**
- Moloco = AI-first mobile DSP, private ($2.1B valuation est. 2025)
- Moloco has been winning accounts from AppLovin with COMPARABLE ROAS claims
- Korean mobile gaming ecosystem (Netmarble, Nexon, Krafton) increasingly using Moloco
- If Moloco raises capital / goes public: Competitive pressure intensifies
- Emma did NOT mention Moloco in her analysis

**Challenge 5C: SDK dependence = vulnerability to OS-level changes**
- AppLovin's entire business depends on Apple + Google allowing SDK-based tracking
- Apple ATT (App Tracking Transparency) 2021 = partial disruption, APP adapted
- But: Google Privacy Sandbox for Android (2026-2027 implementation) could disrupt Android tracking
- If Google implements Android ATT equivalent → APP's Android revenue (est. 60% of total) at risk
- **This is THE existential regulatory risk, not just FTC/GDPR**

**Bear Moat Conclusion: NARROW only (NOT Narrow+)**
