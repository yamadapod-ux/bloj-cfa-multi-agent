Emma's unqualified "WIDE" credits a durability the capital-allocation math and the TXN cost curve do not support. ### 3(a) ⚠️ NEW — Active MOFCOM anti-dumping investigation explicitly names ADI. Nobody priced this as a dated catalyst.

[CFA L3: Country / Political Risk — Trade Policy]

- **13 Sept 2025:** China's Ministry of Commerce opened an **anti-dumping investigation into US-origin analog chips**, naming **Broadcom, Texas Instruments, onsemi, and ADI** ([TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2025/09/15/news-china-opens-analog-chip-dumping-probe-against-ti-broadcom-and-others-over-300-margin/); [SemiMedia](https://www.semimedia.cc/19882.html); [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/china-launches-anti-dumping-probe-into-us-analog-chips)).
- **Scope:** interface & gate-driver ICs on ≥40nm legacy nodes — RS-485/CAN transceivers, digital isolators, I2C expanders, VRM gate drivers. **This is core ADI/TXN catalog analog**, and it overlaps the AI-data-center power content (VRM gate drivers) that is the bull thesis.
- **Allegation:** dumping margin **>300%**; US imports of these parts +37% 2022–24 while average price −52%; ~41% share of China's market.
- **Timeline:** ruling normally completed by **13 September 2026** — i.e. **~2 weeks after this analysis date**; extendable 6 months.
- **Compounding:** TXN's 3 price hikes in 12 months are explicitly cited as "opening a triple-bonanza window for China's domestic substitution" — **SG Micro / 3Peak** have already qualified automotive-grade, signal-chain, isolation and driver parts ([BigGo](https://finance.biggo.com/news/P0vaJZ4B2jrwCtglxZ_n); [Futu](https://news.futunn.com/en/post/62065965/the-ministry-of-commerce-has-launched-an-anti-dumping-investigation)).
- China was **+34% YoY in FY2025** ($2.14B→$2.86B), ADI's single biggest growth driver — **the most at-risk revenue is also the fastest-growing.**

Emma flagged China as "structural risk #2" but did **not** price the ruling as a near-term catalyst. This is a Bear Flip Trigger and belongs in the Catalysts Calendar.
