## Challenges & Debates

### 🚨 Weaknesses in Emma's thesis
1. **ROE 27% in Y1 is too high.** Of that 27%, ~$161M pre-tax (≈ $0.62/share) comes from loan-sale gains in Q1, which are lumpy. Recurring ROE excluding gains is ~21–22%. → Emma tested ROE −2pp in 1.6 (FV $21.84, −6.9%). **But the sensitivity shown does not cover a full gain-on-sale shutdown.** *(Fix #3 — script, v2:)* gain on sale = 0 **for one year** (Y1 ROE 21%) → Emma FV $23.34 → Blended **−$0.31**. But if recurring ROE is −6pp **throughout** (21→17%) → Emma FV **$19.23** → Blended **−$1.95 → $24.61 (MOS +10.2%, below the gate)**.
2. **The 2022 precedent:** FY22 NCO came in at 2.55% vs a 2.3% guide, the stock fell **−16% in one day**, and management admitted the forecast "did not capture" losses ([American Banker/ASR 2023-02-03](https://asreport.americanbanker.com/news/sallie-mae-overhauls-its-credit-analytics-after-charge-offs-jump)). Q2-26 NCO was $113M, while the FY26 guide of $365–385M implies H2 ≈ $82–92M/quarter. **Q3 has to come in sharply lower than Q2 for the guide to hold.** Emma assumes credit stays under control without having seen the print.
3. **Moat "Narrow+" is overstated.** The loan is commodity-priced, the borrower is prime (FICO 755), and can refinance away. Share gains come from rival exits/slow growth, not from pricing power.

### 🚨 Weaknesses in Quinn's analysis
1. **ROE_s 20% forever at g 3% (Base, 50%)** is an aggressive assumption for a monoline lender. Its peers OMF/SYF (ROE 21–23%) are priced at 1.6–1.9× book, not the ~3× that Quinn's model implies. *(Fix #3 v2: if Quinn's probabilities shift to 20/40/40 → Blended $26.56 → **$25.81 (MOS +15.6% — right at the gate)**.)*
2. **The Sharpe/vol estimate is a range-based proxy**, and short interest of 9%+ is high. The −42.8% drawdown within 52 weeks shows the left tail is real.
3. **Equity beta 0.98 understates true risk**, because SLM carries **credit beta** (correlation with HY spreads) that CAPM does not capture → the market prices an implied CoE of ~13% (Quinn's own result). **Additional point in v2:** the bottom-up β (market D/E) gives CoE 7.96%, which pulls the average down to 8.60%. If **Blume only (9.24%)** is used, team MOS = 11.6%, below the gate. **The MOS-gate pass depends on the CoE method** (MOS 15% at CoE 8.93%).
