## CHALLENGE 2: Moat Rating = WIDE may be Overstated [CFA L2: Economic Moat]

**Emma Claimed:** Wide Moat — 5 sources

**Bear Challenges:**

### C2.1 Sanofi Dependency = Moat Limitation

- Dupixent = Regeneron's crown jewel BUT Regeneron gets only ~50% of profits
- Sanofi owns commercialization (sales force, marketing, international distribution)
- If Sanofi faces financial difficulties or strategic shift = REGN Dupixent revenue at risk
- REGN cannot commercialize independently without Sanofi infrastructure globally
- The "Wide Moat" is partially SANOFI's moat, not REGN's alone

### C2.2 JAK Inhibitors = Oral Substitute Threat (Underplayed by Emma)

- Pfizer Cibinqo (abrocitinib), AbbVie Rinvoq (upadacitinib), Eli Lilly Olumiant = oral JAK inhibitors for atopic dermatitis
- Patients prefer oral over injection
- JAK inhibitors gaining share in moderate-to-severe AD, especially younger patients
- Emma's substitution threat rated "MEDIUM" — Bear: should be MED-HIGH for injection Dupixent
- Long-term Dupixent market share at risk from JAK inhibitor penetration

### C2.3 IL-13 Specific Antibodies in Pipeline

- AstraZeneca's cendakimab (IL-33), Amgen's tezepelumab (TSLP) = different pathways but competing in Type 2 inflammation
- New generation antibodies (more selective, potentially better safety profile) in Phase 3
- Dupixent's "first mover" advantage has ~3-5 year window before competition intensifies

**Bear Moat Re-Assessment:** WIDE remains but durability is 7-10 years (not 15-20 years as Emma implies). Adjust DCF terminal period accordingly.
