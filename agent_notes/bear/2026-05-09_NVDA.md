## Challenge Log

### Challenge #1: Revenue CAGR (Emma 32% vs Bear 22%)

**Emma's Claim:** Base CAGR 32% justified by Q1 FY27 $39B Data Center run rate

**Bear's Challenge:**
- Q1 FY27 $39B มาจาก hyperscaler rush to buy Blackwell — pull-forward demand effect
- Comparable: NVDA experienced similar rush with H100 launch (Q2-Q3 FY2024), followed by normalization
- Revenue base FY2026 ~$138B: sustaining 32% CAGR means FY2029E = $253B
- **Is $253B revenue achievable by 2029?** — requires capturing ~65-70% of all AI hardware spend globally
- Global AI semiconductor market FY2029 consensus: ~$350-400B → NVDA at $253B = 63-72% market share
- Bear view: **market share ceiling will hit at ~55-60% — more realistic CAGR = 22%**
- DCF FV at 22% CAGR, WACC 11% = **$115** (vs Emma $148)

**Verdict:** Bear sees CAGR 22-25% as more realistic. Emma's 32% possible in FY2027 but NOT sustainable 5-year average.

## Behavioral Biases Detected

1. **Recency Bias:** Emma and Quinn both upgraded after +35% price recovery from April low. Anchoring to recent positive data vs longer-term cycle risk.
2. **Narrative Fallacy:** "Sovereign AI is a new growth vector" — true, but Bull scenarios already embed this. Double-counting?
3. **Confirmation Bias:** Both agents cited Q1 FY2027 data as "confirming" thesis. But thesis is 3-5 year — one quarter confirms nothing, only reduces short-term uncertainty.
4. **Anchoring:** Both agents anchored to v1 FV ($128/$130) and revised upward, rather than fresh analysis. Independent fresh analysis would give different numbers.
5. **Overconfidence:** Conviction scores both went UP when uncertainty should still be acknowledged — ASIC escalation data points actually increased uncertainty.
