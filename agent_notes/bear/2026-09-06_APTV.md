### 1b. Terminal growth 2.0% is still generous

Emma uses tg 2.0%, Quinn 2.25%. Atlas said "≤2.5%, and do not use >3%" — that is a *ceiling*, not a floor. Arguments for tg **1.5–1.75%**:

- **China share loss is permanent, not paused.** If ~28% of revenue re-bases to local-OEM mix at structurally lower content-per-vehicle *and* lower supplier margin (Atlas §J: ~80% of 2025 China awards were local OEMs, 2/3 of China revenue already local), the terminal revenue base is *smaller* and *lower-margin* than today. That is a one-time step-down that also lowers the sustainable growth rate off the new base.
- **Zonal architecture cannibalizes the content it is supposed to add.** Aptiv's own zonal design studies **eliminate 20+ connectors and multiple ECUs per vehicle and cut harness length 30–40%** ([Tech Briefs — Zonal Electrical Architectures Cut Vehicle Wiring-System Cost, Complexity](https://www.techbriefs.com/component/content/article/49578-zonal-electrical-architectures-cut-vehicle-wiring-system-cost-complexity); [MarketsandMarkets — Future of Vehicle E/E Architecture](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/ResearchInsight/automotive-ee-architecture-market-companies.asp)). RemainCo *kept* the connector business (ECG) and spun the harness — so for the piece Aptiv still sells, the SDV transition is a **mixed-to-negative content event**, not a pure tailwind. See §4.
- **EV-pace slowdown** (Atlas Macro Context 3: US BEV −28% YoY Q1 2026, plug-in share stuck 8–9% through 2027) removes the high-voltage content kicker for the explicit period and pushes the "2–3x content" benefit years out.

Net secular content growth ≈ inflation. **tg 1.75% base.**

## 6. Spin = financial engineering? Margin 18.4% — peak, not floor [CFA L2: Corporate Issuers — Corporate Restructuring]

The bull framing: "RemainCo margin 18.4% >> pre-spin op margin 10.9% → the spin revealed a higher-quality business." **This conflates two different margin metrics and over-credits the carve-out:**

- Pre-spin **GAAP operating** margin ~10.9% vs RemainCo **adjusted EBITDA** margin 18.4% is not an apples-to-apples uplift — it is partly a definitional gap (D&A + adjustments). Pre-spin **adjusted EBITDA** margin was already **18.6–18.7% in 2025** ([Aptiv Q1 2026 transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/05/aptiv-aptv-q1-2026-earnings-transcript/)). On a like-for-like basis, RemainCo's guided 18.4% is **flat to slightly down**, not a step-up.
- The spin took the **$1.9B cash dividend** out of Versigent *before* separation (Atlas §Post-Spin) — RemainCo is the entity that kept the leverage and the **$70M stranded costs**, while the cash went to pay down debt that the dividend itself helped create. Classic "distribute the cash, keep the obligations" structure.
- Management's Q4 margin recovery is a **plan** ("aggressively working to eliminate stranded costs," Q4 inflection) — the same management that missed twice this year.

**Verdict: 18.4% is closer to a cycle peak than a sustainable floor.** Sustainable RemainCo margin, post-China-mix-shift and with realistic stranded-cost stickiness, is **17.0–17.5%**.
