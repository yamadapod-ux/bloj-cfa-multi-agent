## Challenge 1: AI Disruption Risk Seriously Underestimated [CFA L2: Industry Analysis — Disruptive Innovation]

### Emma's View: "AI helps ACN (implementation demand)"
### Bear's Counter:

Emma และ Quinn ใช้ logic ว่า Cloud didn't kill IT consulting — AI won't either. **Bear challenges this analogy:**

1. **Cloud = platform, AI = replacement.** Cloud required MIGRATION (consulting work). AI = AUTONOMOUS EXECUTION — removes the migration need for certain tasks. ChatGPT can do requirements gathering. Cursor AI can write code. What does a junior ACN consultant do?

2. **ACN headcount model = cost structure problem.** ACN charges by the head — 780,000 employees = fixed cost base. If AI allows 1 consultant to do the work of 5, ACN must either: (a) reduce headcount (restructuring cost + social risk) or (b) price down (margin compression). Neither is bullish.

3. **The Bookings ATH Trap.** $22.1B bookings ATH could be LAST CYCLE of traditional IT transformation before hyperscaler AI bundles dominate. Clients rushing to lock in human consulting BEFORE AI displaces it = pull-forward demand, not new structural growth.

4. **Big Tech Bundling = Structural Headwind.** Microsoft Copilot bundled with Azure + M365 licenses → "free" AI implementation. Google Cloud + Gemini bundled. AWS Bedrock + Consulting credits. Why hire ACN at $500/hour when Microsoft includes "implementation support" in enterprise agreement?

**Bear's Revised Revenue Growth View:**
- FY27: +6% (momentum from ATH bookings)
- FY28: +4% (AI displacement accelerates)
- FY29: +2% (structural headcount reduction visible)
- FY30: +2% (new steady state — smaller, more AI-augmented)
- CAGR FY27–30: **3.5%** (vs Emma's 8%)

## Challenge 2: Margin Expansion Assumption is Backwards [CFA L2: Operating Leverage]

### Emma's View: EBIT margin expanding to 16.5–18.5%
### Bear's Counter:

**ACN's margin story is CHALLENGED by 3 forces simultaneously:**
1. **AI talent cost inflation:** Top AI/ML consultants commanding $300–500K+/year — ACN must pay up or lose them; this compresses margins even as AI grows
2. **Pricing pressure:** Clients know AI reduces consultant hours → negotiate lower rates; "AI discount" = structural pricing headwind
3. **Transition cost:** Retraining 780,000 people for AI-augmented work = CAPEX + OPEX surge in FY27–28

Bear revised margin: 14.0–15.0% (vs Emma 16.5–18.5%)

**Impact on DCF:** Bear DCF with 3.5% CAGR + 14.5% margin + 10.5% WACC = **$145/share**
