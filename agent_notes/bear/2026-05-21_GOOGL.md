## Challenge #1 — Net Income $62.58B: What Is Actually In This Number?

**Emma identified this anomaly correctly — Bear wants to push further.**

Analysis of the anomaly:
- Revenue $109.9B × Operating Margin 36.1% = Operating Income **$39.7B**
- Net Income $62.58B = Net Margin **57%** — impossible without large below-the-line items
- Difference: $62.58B − $39.7B = **$22.9B** below-the-line income needed

**Bear's hypothesis:** This was likely a **massive unrealized investment gain** in Alphabet's Other Comprehensive Income or "Other Income" from equity investments. Alphabet holds $12B+ in equity investments in startups (Waymo external, Verily partial, DeepMind spinoff value, etc.) that could have been marked-to-market OR there was a large tax benefit.

**Why This Matters for Valuation:**
- If $22.9B was one-time → FY2026 run-rate EPS much lower than consensus $14.22 implies
- FY2026 consensus $14.22 × 12.25B shares = $174B net income for FY2026 → that's HIGHER than $62.58B × 4 = $250B annualized Q1 → consensus must be excluding one-time
- **Bear conclusion:** Consensus $14.22 probably already normalized; Emma's normalized $11–12 EPS may actually be too conservative for full year

**Bear resolution:** Use consensus $14.22 for FY2026 as it's analyst-normalized, but flag Q1 $62.58B as NOT usable for forward extrapolation.
