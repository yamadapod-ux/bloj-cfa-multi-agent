## Key Regulatory Data Points

### DOJ Antitrust Investigation
- **Status:** Active investigation (as of 2025); DOJ issued civil investigative demands
- **Target:** FICO's per-score pricing practices — from ~$0.50 (2013) to ~$3.50 (2024) = 7x increase
- **Potential outcomes (Atlas probability assessment):**
  - Status quo (no action): 25%
  - Price cap / consent decree: 45%
  - Structural remedy (forced divestiture or competitor creation): 20%
  - Full antitrust case / settlement with major concessions: 10%
- **Financial Impact (Bear-level scenarios):**
  - Price cap at $2.00/score: Revenue impact ~-$200-300M/yr (-10-15%)
  - Structural remedy: Revenue impact ~-30-40%

### FHFA VantageScore Reform
- **Status:** VantageScore 4.0 approved as alternative to FICO for GSE loans (effective 2025)
- **Implementation pace:** Gradual — lenders need 12–24 months to adapt systems
- **Bi-merge vs tri-merge:** Reduces tri-bureau pulls → reduces total FICO pulls by ~15%
- **FICO counter:** FICO Score 10T certified for bi-merge use as well
- **Net assessment:** ~5-10% long-term market share risk in mortgage channel
