## Section J: Customer Concentration (Full Detail)

**TurboTax (Consumer):**
- Customer type: Individual tax filers — B2C model
- ~47M units filed in FY2025
- No customer >1%
- IRS Direct File: 25 states (2025) → 40+ states (2026) = structural risk to free tier (~70% of all TurboTax users file free/Simple)
- TurboTax Paid tier: ~14M filers (avg $69–$119 filing cost)
- TurboTax Live: ~2.5M filers (avg $269–$419) → fastest growing, highest margin
- **Concentration Risk: VERY LOW**

**QuickBooks Online (SMB):**
- 10.1M paying subscribers globally
- No single customer >$1M/yr likely
- Industry spread: 22% professional services, 18% construction/trades, 15% retail, 12% restaurant/food, 10% healthcare, 23% other
- Average Revenue Per User (ARPU): ~$450/yr (growing from $320 in FY2022)
- **Concentration Risk: VERY LOW**

**Credit Karma (Marketplace):**
- Revenue from financial institution advertisers/partners
- Personal Finance Lead Gen Partners: Top 5 card issuers (Capital One, Chase, Citi, Amex, Discover) = estimated 40–50% of CK card revenue
- Mortgage Partners: Rocket/Quicken, UWM, Better.com = estimated 35–45% of CK mortgage revenue
- Auto Insurance Partners: Progressive, Geico, State Farm = major contributors
- **Concentration Risk: MEDIUM** — financial partner marketing budgets cyclical + concentrated
- CK vulnerability: if macro credit tightening → lenders reduce partner marketing → CK revenue falls significantly (happened 2022-2023: CK declined ~15%)

**Net Concentration Assessment:**
- Portfolio-weighted: ~60% revenue (QB+TT) = VERY LOW concentration
- ~11% revenue (CK) = MEDIUM concentration → drag during credit downturns
- **Blended overall: LOW-MEDIUM**

## Section L: AI/Technology Context

**Intuit AI Strategy:**
1. **Intuit Assist** — GenAI assistant embedded in TurboTax, QuickBooks, Credit Karma, Mailchimp
2. **Anthropic Partnership** — Claude-powered AI agents for financial workflow automation (announced 2025)
3. **Intuit Data Platform** — 100M+ consumer + SMB financial profiles → proprietary training data moat
4. **AI-powered bookkeeping** — 70M+ financial transactions processed daily → ML categorization accuracy
5. **AI in TurboTax** — Smart tax recommendations, audit risk scoring, deduction optimization

**AI Risk to INTU:**
- Free LLM-powered tax prep (ChatGPT, Claude) could replace simple DIY TurboTax
- BUT: INTU's moat = data (100M profiles) + trust (IRS accuracy guarantee) + state e-filing integrations
- Intuit has MORE data advantage than any competitor or LLM provider
- INTU can integrate LLMs (Anthropic) while maintaining data moat = defensive AND offensive
