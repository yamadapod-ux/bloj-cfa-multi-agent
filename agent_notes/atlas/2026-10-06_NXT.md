## Sector context — Solar/Renewable Energy (Round 73 continuation)
- Sector-wide 2026 dislocation driven by (1) elevated 10Y Treasury yields (~5.0-5.3%, Bloomberg/Reuters, highest since 2002) compressing long-duration growth-equity valuations sector-wide, (2) residential-solar financing stress (mortgage/HELOC-rate sensitivity hitting ENPH/SEDG), (3) the Section 25D residential ITC expiration (Dec 31, 2025, OBBBA) — a **structural** hit specific to residential-lease/loan models (RUN), and (4) tariff-cost-pass-through timing uncertainty hitting utility-scale tracker-hardware names (NXT, ARRY) on a margin-optics basis even where underlying demand is intact.
- **NXT is utility-scale/commercial, NOT residential** — the Section 25D ITC expiration that structurally impairs RUN does **not** apply to NXT's business model. NXT's core policy tailwind is the **IRA Section 45X Advanced Manufacturing Production Credit** (domestic content credit), which contributed ~$99M to Q1 FY27 results alone (per Nextpower 8-K, Jul 30, 2026) — a live, utilized, not-yet-expired credit.
- **Tariff mechanics (IEEPA, Section 301, AD/CVD on imported solar cells/modules from Southeast Asia):** NXT's Q1 FY27 gross margin of 37% (vs. "low-30s" target) was explicitly flagged by management as benefiting from **tariff recoveries** (i.e., NXT is currently a net beneficiary of the tariff regime via pass-through pricing + domestic manufacturing incentives), not a victim of it. This is a double-edged policy exposure: if domestic-content/tariff policy support is reduced or reversed under a future administration or trade-deal resolution, NXT's current above-target margins could compress back toward the "low-30s" organic target — a real risk Bear must weigh, but it is a **margin-normalization risk, not a structural-impairment risk** (the "low-30s" organic target still implies a profitable, going-concern business).
- **Macro scenario stress test (per CLAUDE.md §Macro Scenario Stress Test, 1-2 relevant scenarios):**
  1. **Fed hike / yields stay elevated (most relevant given current ~5.0-5.3% 10Y):** Higher-for-longer rates keep WACC elevated (NXT's own beta-implied CAPM cost of equity is ~14.6-14.8% per stockanalysis.com's computed WACC of 14.79% — well above the 7-13% "normal" DCF range) — this directly compresses DCF-based FV for a capital-intensive, beta-1.9 cyclical name. Emma uses a WACC within the 10-13% QA-sanity band for the primary case (justified below) but flags that a strict CAPM read would be higher.
  2. **Tariff/policy reversal scenario (NXT-specific):** If Section 301/IEEPA tariff recoveries are negotiated away or 45X credit value erodes, gross margin could mean-revert from 37% to the "low-30s" organic target — a ~400-700bps margin hit modeled explicitly in Emma's Bear-case DCF scenario below (margin held flat at 13% FCF margin vs. base case's 15.5-17.5% expansion path).
