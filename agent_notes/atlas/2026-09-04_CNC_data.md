## H. Key Business Metrics — Centene-Specific (สำหรับ Emma's Business Deep Dive)

### H1. 2025 ACA Guidance Withdrawal Crisis — Full Detail
- **What happened:** On **July 1, 2025**, Centene withdrew its FY2025 guidance after actuarial firm Wakely reported ACA Marketplace exchange enrollment was lower and morbidity (health-risk level of the risk pool) was significantly higher than Centene had projected in its original guidance.
- **Scope of data:** Preliminary Wakely data covered 22 of Centene's 27 exchange states (~72% of ACA membership), showing enrollment growth below expectations and market morbidity significantly exceeding the assumptions embedded in prior 2025 guidance.
- **Quantified financial impact:** ~**$1.8 billion** reduction to expected 2025 net risk-adjustment revenue transfer, equating to **~$2.75 of adjusted EPS**, or **~38% of the prior EPS guidance of >$7.25**.
- **Root cause:** Program-integrity changes ahead of the 2025 open-enrollment period discouraged healthier enrollees from signing up (adverse selection), shifting the risk pool toward higher morbidity. Medicaid redeterminations also pushed higher-acuity individuals onto ACA exchanges, compounding the mix shift.
- **Resulting FY2025 results:** Full-year 2025 net loss of -$6,674M (EPS -$13.53), with Q4 2025 alone reportedly near a ~$253M-scale quarterly loss pattern continuing from mid-year (exact Q4 loss not independently re-verified this session — Emma should pull the Q4 2025/FY2025 earnings press release directly if needed for the exact quarterly bridge).
- **Recovery status (as of Q2 2026 report, July 28, 2026):** Company describes itself as "back on track." Q1 2026 and Q2 2026 were both solidly profitable ($1.09B net income in Q2 2026 alone, adjusted EPS $2.51 vs a Q2 2025 adjusted EPS of -$0.16). FY2026 guidance raised twice this year (most recently at Q2 to >$4.80 adjusted EPS). Marketplace segment 2026 pretax margin guidance lifted to 4.5–5.0%, aided by a **$180M favorable 2025 CMS risk-adjustment reconciliation** recognized in Q2 2026 (i.e., part of the recovery is a one-time catch-up reconciliation, not pure ongoing operating improvement — Emma should separate the recurring vs. one-time components).
- Sources: [Modern Healthcare](https://www.modernhealthcare.com/insurance/mh-centene-guidance-aca-risk-adjustment/), [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/analyst-ratings/centene-stock-plunges-as-company-withdraws-2025-guidance-on-aca-exchange-concerns-93CH-4120616), [FierceHealthcare](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/payers/centene-posts-253m-loss-amid-aca-marketplace-woes), [Centene Q2 2026 press release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/centene-corporation-reports-second-quarter-2026-results-302835878.html)

### H3. Medicaid Redetermination ("Unwinding") Status
- The pandemic-era continuous-coverage unwinding process (began April 2023) is **largely complete** entering 2026 — states substantially finished the backlog of eligibility redeterminations by end of 2025.
- **New structural change for 2026 forward (via the One Big Beautiful Bill Act / OBBBA):** Expansion-population adults (ages 19–64, non-disabled) must now meet **work requirements** in states implementing them, and starting **December 31, 2026**, this population's Medicaid eligibility will be redetermined **every 6 months instead of annually** (vs. traditional populations — children, pregnant women, elderly, disabled — which remain on annual redetermination).
- **Rollout timeline:** Nebraska began enforcing work requirements May 1, 2026; Montana and Iowa plan enforcement by end of 2026; all states required to comply by January 1, 2027.
- **Implication for CNC:** The original 2023–2025 "unwinding shock" (membership losses as ineligible enrollees were purged) is largely behind the company, but a **new, structurally faster churn cycle** (6-month redeterminations + work requirements) begins at the very end of 2026 and ramps through 2027 — this is a **forward-looking secular headwind to flag as a Bear Flip Trigger candidate**, not a resolved issue.
- Sources: [KFF — 10 things to know about unwinding](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/10-things-to-know-about-the-unwinding-of-the-medicaid-continuous-enrollment-provision/), [CoveredUSA 2026 guide](https://coveredusa.org/en/event/medicaid-redetermination-by-state), [BenefitsUSA blog](https://benefitsusa.org/en/blog/medicaid-redetermination-2026)

### H4. ACA Enhanced Premium Tax Credit (PTC) — Legislative Status Update (vs OSCR 2026-09-01 report)
**Confirms and slightly extends** the status Atlas previously reported in the OSCR analysis (2026-09-01):
- Enhanced PTCs (originally from ARPA, extended through 2025 via the Inflation Reduction Act) **expired without Congressional renewal** at year-end 2025. Competing Senate proposals in December 2025 failed to advance.
- Consumer impact realized in 2026: KFF reports average annual premiums paid by subsidized enrollees **more than doubled**, from $888 (2025) to **$1,904 (2026)**.
- **New information found this session (not in the OSCR 09-01 note):** The **Lower Health Care Costs Act (S. 3385)**, which would have extended enhanced PTCs through 2028, **failed to reach the 60-vote Senate threshold**. Separately, in December 2025 the **House voted to support a discharge petition** forcing a House floor vote on a 3-year PTC extension bill, which the House was expected to vote on in **January 2026** — but it remains **unclear/unresolved as of this search (Sept 2026) whether any such bill has passed both chambers**. No confirmation found of further movement in the Sept 1–4, 2026 window specifically — **treat legislative status as unchanged/still-stalled** pending the earlier OSCR scenario tree (cliff ~37% / CARE Act ~41% / clean extension ~17% probability-weighted scenarios).
- **Relevance to CNC:** Centene is a large ACA Marketplace participant — the PTC cliff is a direct headwind to Marketplace enrollment/mix (adverse selection risk: subsidized low-morbidity enrollees drop out first), which is the same dynamic that caused the 2025 crisis (Section H1). This is one of the more important overhangs for Emma/Bear to model explicitly, not just carry over from OSCR without CNC-specific incremental checking.
