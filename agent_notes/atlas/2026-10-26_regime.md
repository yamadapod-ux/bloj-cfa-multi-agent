### Scenario 1: Inflation surprise, Fed hikes Dec 9 + one more (rates/consumer-credit channel)
- Base case already priced: about 75% odds of a Dec hike (DeFi Rate). The tail is a **+50 bp cumulative** move (Dec plus early 2027) with a ~1:1 pass-through to Rf → **10Y ≈ 5.50%**.
- WACC effect for WGO (weights E 64.1% / D 35.9%, t 22.3%, locked base WACC 7.91% from the Data Package): ΔWACC ≈ +50 bp × (0.641 + 0.359 × 0.777 × pass-through to marginal Kd ≈ 1) ≈ **+0.46pp**, from 7.91% to about 8.37%. For a DCF with TGR 2.5%, a +0.46pp move cuts terminal value by roughly 8% (the WACC−g spread goes from 5.41% to 5.87%).
- The demand side matters more than WACC. Prime is 7.00% today, so dealer floorplan (mostly SOFR/prime-linked) would rise another +50 bp. Credit-union RV/marine loan APRs (6.35–7.44% in Aug–Sep 2026: [Summit FCU](https://www.summitfcu.org/loans/recreational-vehicle-rv-loans/), [Mayo CU](https://www.mayocreditunion.org/rates/vehicle.html)) would rise 0.5pp, and monthly payments on a 10–15 year loan would rise about 3–4%. That puts more pressure on dealer destocking, which is the main driver of WGO's towable softness (Q4 FY26 towables −28.1%).
- Indicator: if HY OAS widens past 400 at the same time, 2 indicators move to TC and the regime call becomes a judgment.
