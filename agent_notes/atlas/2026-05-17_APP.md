### F. Business Model

**Revenue Segments:**
1. **Software Platform (~76% of revenue, growing):** AXON AI engine — connects mobile app developers (supply side) with advertisers (demand side). Revenue = % of ad spend flowing through platform (take rate ~20-25%)
2. **Apps (~24% of revenue, declining):** Portfolio of ~200 mobile games (owned). Being explored for spin-off/sale. Declining margin contributor

**Unit Economics:**
- Take Rate: ~20-25% of gross ad spend
- Gross Margin (Software): ~88%
- Gross Margin (Apps): ~15-20%
- Blended Gross Margin Q1 2026: ~88% (implies Apps contribution minimal to margin)

**AXON 2.0 AI Engine:**
- Core differentiator: Proprietary ML model trained on billions of mobile ad auctions
- Advantage: Real-time bidding optimization → higher ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) for advertisers
- Flywheel: More ad spend → more data → better model → more ad spend
