## I. DOJ Antitrust Analysis (Critical Update สำหรับ v2)

### Case Summary
- **Case:** US DOJ v. Visa Inc. (2024)
- **Allegation:** Visa monopolizes US debit card processing market by requiring banks to route debit transactions through Visa network via exclusive agreements — stifles FedNow/RTP competition
- **Legal Basis:** Sherman Antitrust Act Section 2 (monopolization)
- **Visa Defense:** FedNow is young; Visa provides superior service; competition exists (PIN debit networks — NYCE, PULSE, Star)

### Timeline Scenarios (Atlas estimate 2026-05-11)

| Timeline | Event |
|----------|-------|
| Q2-Q3 2026 | Pre-trial motions, discovery continues |
| Q4 2026 – Q1 2027 | Trial expected to begin |
| Q2-Q4 2027 | District court ruling expected |
| 2028+ | Appeals process (if ruling against Visa) |

### Comparable Cases (for probability calibration)

| Case | Outcome | Lessons |
|------|---------|---------|
| DOJ v. Google Search (2024) | **DOJ WON** — Structural remedy discussions | DOJ willing to pursue aggressively |
| DOJ v. Apple (2024) | Ongoing | Pattern of strong DOJ action |
| DOJ v. Visa/Mastercard (2010) | **Settlement** — behavioral remedy | Prior case = consent decree |
| DOJ v. American Express (2018) | **DOJ LOST at Supreme Court** | Antitrust hard to prove in two-sided markets |

**Atlas Probability Estimate (updated):**
- DOJ ถอน/แพ้: 35% (เพิ่มจาก 30% ใน v1 — AmEx precedent + debit specificity)
- Settlement behavioral: 40% (ลดจาก 45%)
- Structural remedy (full win): 25% (ลดจาก 35% ใน Bear v1 — debit-only scope limits damage)
