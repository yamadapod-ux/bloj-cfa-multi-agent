### 💡 Macro Implications for WGO
**Rate Sensitivity:** **High, through two channels.** (a) Demand: retail buyers use long-term loans (8–15 years per the credit-union rate sheets above; the share of buyers who finance is DATA NOT AVAILABLE), and dealers carry floorplan. (b) Valuation: WACC is sensitive to Rf (ΔWACC ≈ +0.23pp per +25 bp). The balance sheet itself is not very rate-sensitive: 100% of debt is fixed (senior secured 6.25% due 2028, convertible 3.25% due 2030), and the ABL is undrawn.
**Dollar Sensitivity:** **Low.** US revenue is 93.9%. Tariffs and import costs (China/Mexico/Canada) matter more than FX.
**Cycle Position:** Trough. Revenue fell −45% from the FY22 peak and EBIT −89%, and FY27 guides flat revenue ($2.65–2.80B). This is the cycle position where a DCF must use **mid-cycle**, not current earnings and not peak earnings.
