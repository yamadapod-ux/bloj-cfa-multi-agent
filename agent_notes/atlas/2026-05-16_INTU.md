## C. Macro Risks Specific to INTU

### HIGH Priority Risks
1. **Free Tax Filing Expansion (IRS Direct File)**
   - IRS Direct File program expanded to 25 states in 2025, 40+ in 2026
   - Direct threat to TurboTax Free tier — forces INTU to compete on features not price
   - Probability of major disruption: 30–40% (3–5yr horizon)

2. **SMB Credit Cycle Tightening**
   - Consumer credit delinquency rising to 8.5%
   - Credit Karma insurance/personal loan lead volume declining
   - Credit Karma FY2025 revenue growth: ~2% (vs peak ~15%)

3. **AI Disruption to Guided Tax Prep**
   - LLMs (GPT-4, Claude, Gemini) can handle simple-to-moderate tax returns
   - Threat to TurboTax's value proposition for premium tiers
   - Intuit's response: Intuit Assist + Anthropic partnership

### MEDIUM Priority Risks
4. **Rate Environment Impact on Credit Karma**
   - High rates suppress mortgage origination volume (-60% from 2021 peak)
   - Credit card origination slowing
   - Direct impact: Credit Karma revenue tied to financial product origination

5. **H&R Block Digital Acceleration**
   - HRB Digital growing faster than INTU in assisted category
   - TurboTax Live (human assisted) direct competitive response

## J. Customer Concentration Analysis

- No single customer >1% of revenue
- B2C model — highly diversified customer base
- Top 5 customer type: DIY filers, Small business owners (Schedule C), Investors (capital gains), Self-employed, Military filers
- Concentration risk: LOW

**QuickBooks (Small Business Segment):**
- ~7.5M paying subscribers globally (FY2025 est.)
- No single customer material
- Top industries: Professional services, Construction, Retail, Restaurants, Healthcare practices
- Concentration risk: VERY LOW (SaaS subscription model, highly fragmented SMB base)

**Credit Karma:**
- Lead generation model — revenue from financial institutions
- Top credit card partners: Capital One, Chase, Citi, Discover, Amex
- Top mortgage partners: Quicken/Rocket, UWM, Caliber
- **Concentration risk: MEDIUM** — top 5 financial institutions likely represent 35–45% of Credit Karma revenue
- If any top credit partner reduces marketing spend → Credit Karma revenue impact

**Overall Customer Concentration: LOW-MEDIUM**
- TurboTax + QuickBooks = low concentration (B2C/SMB fragmented)
- Credit Karma = medium concentration (B2B lead gen, financial institution partners)
