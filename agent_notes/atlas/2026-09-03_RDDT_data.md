### H2. EU DSA / VLOP Designation — status update since 2026-08-31
Confirmed: European Commission designated Reddit (alongside ChatGPT, Roblox) as a **Very Large Online Platform (VLOP)** under the Digital Services Act on **August 31, 2026**. Reddit reported **57.2M average monthly EU users** as of July 2026 (this is the basis for the designation — DSA VLOP threshold is 45M+ EU MAU). **Compliance timeline: 4 months from notification → compliance obligations due by ~January 2027** (systemic risk assessments, algorithmic audits, minor-protection mechanisms, illegal-content mechanisms). **Penalty for non-compliance: up to 6% of global annual turnover** (unchanged from Emma's 2026-09-01 note — confirmed, no new development since designation itself). Source: [European Commission "Commission designates ChatGPT, Reddit, Roblox under DSA"](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-designates-chatgpt-reddit-roblox-under-digital-services-act), [Deloitte "Clock started: EU DSA VLOP/VLOSE designations announced"](https://www.deloitte.com/in/en/services/consulting-risk/blogs/clock-started-eu-dsa-vlop-vlose-designations-announced.html)

### H3. ChatGPT/AI-search referral traffic — status update since 2026-09-01
No material new development since the -86% collapse already captured in Emma's 2026-09-01 note. Additional context found this session: the collapse (Reddit's ChatGPT Search citation share 3.83%→0.52%, Jul 18–Aug 17 window) coincided with ChatGPT increasing its use of `site:`-qualified narrow-domain search fanout queries (0.4%→~17% of queries), which mechanically excludes broad Reddit threads from the candidate pool. **Importantly: the lost citation share has migrated to YouTube, Wikipedia, and Facebook (via Google AI overviews) — not evenly redistributed** — and **Perplexity citations of Reddit roughly doubled over the same window** (Perplexity now supplies 71% of tracked Reddit AI citations vs. ChatGPT's 23%, of 44,488 citations tracked Jul 1–Aug 19). **Historical precedent flagged by sources: an near-identical collapse occurred in September 2025 and fully recovered** — this may not be a permanent structural loss, tempering (not negating) the bear case. Source: [On-Page.ai "Reddit lost 86% of ChatGPT citations"](https://blog.on-page.ai/reddit-lost-86-chatgpt-citations-heres/), [Pierview "Reddit Citations Fell Off a Cliff in ChatGPT, While Perplexity Doubled Down"](https://www.pierview.ai/guides/reddit-citations-chatgpt-perplexity-decline-2026), [TryAivo "Reddit's ChatGPT Citations Fell 86%.
