## Sector Context: Financial Analytics / Credit Scoring

### Industry Structure
- **FICO Score Penetration:** ~90% of US consumer lending decisions use FICO Score
- **Competitive Moat Assessment:** Near-monopoly in mortgage (GSE-mandated), duopoly in consumer credit with VantageScore
- **Pricing Power:** FICO raised per-score prices 2022–2024 (avg price increase ~35%) — primary revenue driver
- **B2B vs B2C Revenue Mix:** B2B (Scores segment) ~70% / Software platform ~30%
- **End Market Sensitivity:** Mortgage origination = single largest FICO revenue driver (~20-25% of Scores revenue)

### Regulatory Landscape (Critical for FICO)
- **DOJ Investigation:** Antitrust probe into FICO Score pricing practices (announced ~2022, active 2024–2025)
  - Focus: Price increases from ~$0.50 to ~$3.50 per score (7x in decade) — DOJ examining market power abuse
  - Potential Outcomes: Price rollback (base case), structural remedy, settlement with conditions
  - Precedent: DOJ has successfully prosecuted payment network cases (Visa 2024 debit network)
- **FHFA VantageScore Push:**
  - FHFA approved VantageScore 4.0 as alternative for Fannie/Freddie (effective 2024–2025 implementation)
  - Bi-merge credit report now accepted (vs tri-merge before) — reduces total pulls
  - Impact: Structural headwind to mortgage volumes and pricing power
  - Not eliminated FICO — dual-score now (FICO 10T required alongside VantageScore in some contexts)
- **Consumer Financial Protection:** CFPB focus on "credit invisibles" — alternative data scoring push

## Macro Implications for FICO Analysis

### Tailwinds
1. **Interest rate cuts in H2 2026** (if materialized) = mortgage origination recovery = FICO volume uplift
2. **Consumer credit demand rising** — economic stress increases credit application volume
3. **Software platform expansion** — FICO Platform (DaaS) growing above average vs cyclical Scores segment
4. **International expansion** — ~25% revenue from international, growing faster than US

1. **Sustained high rates** = mortgage origination suppression continues

3. **FHFA VantageScore implementation** = market share erosion in mortgage channel
4. **AI/ML alternative data** models gaining institutional acceptance (fintech lenders)
