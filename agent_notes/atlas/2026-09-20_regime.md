The 2026-09-11 record explicitly staged the conditions for a flip back to RISK-ON: *"High probability of flip back to RISK-ON if (a) FOMC holds or hikes-and-signals-done with dovish guidance, and (b) August CPI core comes in ≤0.2% MoM."* Reviewing what actually happened:

This is confirmation of a hawkish tightening *cycle*, not a one-and-done event. The specific de-escalation trigger the prior record set for itself did not fire.

Wednesday's announcement triggered a **−600pt Dow drop**; Thursday–Friday recovered on relief (clear Fed communication, easing Fed-independence fears) but the week closed net risk-averse outside of large-cap tech. This is a "relief bounce that avoided the worst case," not genuine broad risk appetite.
4. **This is a first-hike-in-3+-years structural regime shift**, not a one-off surprise — the discount-rate backdrop for equities (especially debt-heavy, rate-sensitive balance sheets — directly relevant to today's LVS analysis, net debt $11.9B) has genuinely changed direction from an extended pause/cut-bias world to an active tightening cycle with more hikes explicitly guided.

Credit and volatility markets show **zero** stress signal — this rules out RISK-OFF. The hawkish Fed pivot is a real but *contained* headwind, not a crisis signal.

**Net judgment:** the majority-vote 3/4 RISK-ON reading is built on three coincident/lagging indicators (VIX, curve, credit) that have not yet been tested by the *consequences* of a multi-hike tightening cycle still ahead (dot plot implies 1–2 more hikes into year-end).
