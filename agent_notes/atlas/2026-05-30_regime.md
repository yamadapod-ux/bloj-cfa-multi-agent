3. **Platform moat:** User-generated content (UGC) across 100,000+ communities — authentic human conversation that AI models need for training. Network effects strong (niche communities self-reinforcing).

1. **Meta "Forum" app** (launched May 22, 2026): Direct community discussion competitor. Meta has 3B+ MAU distribution advantage.
2. **Revenue concentration:** 94% advertising — ad market cyclicality risk.
3. **Google dependency:** $60M/year data deal + Google drives significant referral traffic. If Google AI Search reduces Reddit visibility, both ad and licensing revenue threatened.
4. **Insider selling** noted in late May.
5. **Profitability track record:** First GAAP profit only in 2025 — limited operating leverage history.
6. **Regulatory fine** noted (May 22) — nature unknown from available sources.
