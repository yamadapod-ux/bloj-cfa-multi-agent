## Methodology

**Framework chosen:** Per CLAUDE.md Insurer Valuation Framework (precedent: OSCR 2026-09-01, ACGL/PGR sessions) — standard DCF/EV-Revenue is **not used** for a health insurer whose cash flows are distorted by reserve/working-capital timing and a one-off crisis year. Primary valuation lens:

1. **Insurance Residual Income / Justified P/B–ROE model** [CFA L2: Equity Valuation — Residual Income / Justified Multiples] — `Justified P/B = (ROE − g) / (r − g)`, applied to **normalized** (not TTM GAAP) ROE
2. **Normalized forward P/E cross-check**, using recurring (ex-one-time) EPS vs. peer multiples

TTM GAAP figures (Net Income -$5.1B, ROE -20.4%) are explicitly **not used** for valuation — they are dominated by the H2-2025 ACA guidance-withdrawal crisis. Instead, valuation is built from **FY2026 guidance** (adjusted EPS >$4.80, GAAP EPS >$3.11), stripped of the one-time Q2 2026 item, consistent with Atlas's explicit instruction in the Data Package.

## Full Calculations

### 1. Normalizing FY2026 Earnings [CFA L1: Financial Reporting — Non-Recurring Item Adjustment]

- FY2026 guidance (raised at Q2 2026 report, 2026-07-28): **Adjusted EPS >$4.80** (floor/minimum guide, not a point estimate) [Source: Centene Q2 2026 press release, PRNewswire]
- This guide **includes a one-time $180M favorable 2025 CMS risk-adjustment reconciliation** recognized in Q2 2026 — a prior-year catch-up, not recurring operating improvement [Source: Atlas Data Package Section H1, citing Centene Q2 2026 press release]
- One-time benefit per share: $180M / 494.0M shares = **$0.36/share**
- **Normalized recurring FY2026 adjusted EPS = $4.80 − $0.36 = $4.44** *(this is the base used for the conservative valuation case below)*

### 2. Normalized ROE (DuPont cross-check) [CFA L1: Financial Reporting — DuPont Decomposition]

Two cases, both using guidance-based (not TTM GAAP) net income:

| Case | Adj. Net Income (494.0M shares) | ÷ Equity ($22,657M) | Normalized ROE |
|------|-------|------|------|
| Conservative (ex one-time item): $4.44 EPS | $2,193M | $22,657M | **9.67%** |
| Base (full guided floor): $4.80 EPS | $2,371M | $22,657M | **10.46%** |

DuPont check (conservative case): Net Margin 1.12% ($2,193M / $195.5B guidance midpoint revenue) × Asset Turnover 2.355x ($195.5B/$83,012M) × Equity Multiplier 3.664x ($83,012M/$22,657M) = **9.67% ROE** ✅ ties out.

BVPS = $22,657M / 494.0M shares = **$45.87/share** [Source: Atlas Data Package Section C, Stockanalysis Balance Sheet]

### 3. Cost of Equity (r) [CFA L1: CAPM]

`r = Rf + β × ERP = 4.78% + 1.11 × 4.5% = 9.78%`
- Rf = 4.78% (10Y Treasury) [Source: Atlas Data Package Section F, MacroMicro]
- β = 1.11 (5Y monthly) [Source: Atlas Data Package Section F, Yahoo Finance]
- ERP = 4.5% *(team assumption — Damodaran-style long-run US equity risk premium; not sourced from a live query this session, flagged as assumption)*

### 4. WACC (for ROIC quality check only — not used as r in the P/B model) [CFA L1/L2: Cost of Capital]

- Cost of debt (pre-tax) ≈ Rf + ~1.2% credit spread ≈ 5.98% → after-tax (21% tax) ≈ **4.72%**
- Weights (market value): E = $33.60B mkt cap (67.6%), D = $16.105B total debt (32.4%) [Source: Atlas Data Package Section A, C]
- **WACC = 0.676 × 9.78% + 0.324 × 4.72% ≈ 8.14%**

### 5. Normalized ROIC vs. WACC [CFA L2: ROIC/EVA Framework]

- Invested Capital = Debt + Equity = $16,105M + $22,657M = $38,762M *(cash NOT netted out — per Atlas's explicit flag, most of CNC's $24.1B cash backs statutory/regulatory reserves, not freely deployable corporate cash)*
- NOPAT proxy (conservative, ex one-time item) = $2,193M → **Normalized ROIC ≈ 5.66%**
- NOPAT proxy (GAAP guidance floor, $3.11 EPS × 494M) = $1,537M → **Normalized ROIC ≈ 3.97%**
- **Normalized ROIC range ≈ 4.0%–5.7%, vs. WACC ≈ 8.1%** → **ROIC < WACC even after normalizing for the 2025 one-time loss.** This resolves the Atlas-flagged ROIC dispersion (4.32% vs. 8.77%) — the true picture sits closer to the low end once TTM distortion is corrected. **This is a real quality flag, not just a TTM artifact** — CNC is not clearly compounding economic value at the invested-capital level, consistent with a thin-margin, government-payer-driven business model.

### 6. Justified P/B (Insurance Residual Income Model) [CFA L2: Equity Valuation — Residual Income / Justified P/B]

`Justified P/B = (ROE − g) / (r − g)`, g = 3.5% *(assumption — long-run sustainable growth for a Medicaid/ACA-exposed MCO facing two structural headwinds; roughly GDP+ growth, deliberately conservative given PTC cliff + redetermination headwinds below)*

| Case | ROE | (ROE − g) | (r − g) | Justified P/B | FV ($ = P/B × $45.87 BVPS) |
|------|-----|-----------|---------|---------------|------|
| Conservative (ex one-time) | 9.67% | 6.17% | 6.28% | 0.982x | **$45.05** |
| Base (full guided floor) | 10.46% | 6.96% | 6.28% | 1.108x | **$50.83** |

### 7. Normalized Forward P/E Cross-Check [CFA L2: Relative Valuation]

Applying a peer-informed multiple **below** the sector median (16.59x) — justified because normalized ROE (9.67-10.46%) sits below what a "quality" multiple would command, and because CI (9.03x fwd P/E), the closest quality analog (both still carrying a "recovering from crisis" discount), anchors the low end:

- 10x × $4.44 normalized EPS = **$44.40**
- 11x × $4.44 normalized EPS = **$48.84**
- Midpoint ≈ **$46.62**

**⚠️ Key finding — the "cheap" headline forward P/E is partly an illusion:** Current price $68.02 ÷ full guided EPS $4.80 = 14.17x (matches the low end of Atlas's 11.8–14.6x dispersion). But $68.02 ÷ **normalized recurring EPS $4.44** = **15.32x** — this is essentially in line with the sector median (16.59x), not a discount. **Once the one-time $180M reconciliation is stripped out, most of CNC's apparent "cheapness vs. peers" disappears.**

### 8. Blended Fair Value

Average of: Conservative P/B ($45.05) + Base P/B ($50.83) + P/E cross-check midpoint ($46.62) = **$47.50**

**Emma's Fair Value: $47.50** (range $44–$51)

### 9. Margin of Safety at $68.02

`MOS = (FV − Price) / Price = ($47.50 − $68.02) / $68.02 = -30.2%`

**MOS = -30.2% — deeply negative. FAILS Value bucket BUY threshold (needs MOS ≥15%).** For MOS to reach +15% at this FV, price would need to fall to FV/1.15 ≈ **$41.30** — a further ~39% decline from today's $68.02, closer to (but still above) the 52-week low of $28.24.

### 10. Reverse-Engineered Implied Growth (sanity check) [CFA L2: Reverse DCF/RI Analysis]

Solving `(ROE − g)/(r − g) = current P/B 1.49x` for g, using the generous ROE case (10.46%):
`g = (1.49×9.78% − 10.46%) / 0.49 = 8.39%`

**The market is currently pricing CNC at an implied perpetual growth rate of ~8.4%** — aggressive for a Medicaid-heavy MCO facing two live structural headwinds (ACA PTC cliff, new 6-month Medicaid redetermination cycle starting Dec 2026) and TTM revenue growth (12.9%) that is itself likely to decelerate as membership shrinks. This corroborates the negative MOS finding independently of the P/B/P/E build above.

### 11. Secondary/Confirmatory Metrics (not primary valuation basis)

- EV/EBITDA 5.55x (Atlas primary source) vs. MOH ~7.04x, ELV ~9.65x — also reads "cheap," but Atlas flagged a wide dispersion (5.55x/7.66x/-4.98x across sources) for this metric — **not relied upon** as a standalone signal.

## Business Deep Dive Content (feeds into Report §🏢)

### How does Centene make money?
- รัฐบาล (state Medicaid agencies) และ CMS (federal, สำหรับ Medicare/ACA) จ่ายเบี้ยประกันรายเดือนคงที่ต่อสมาชิก 1 คน (capitated PMPM rate) ให้ Centene ตามสัญญาที่ชนะการประมูล
- Centene รับผิดชอบจ่ายค่ารักษาพยาบาลจริงแทนสมาชิก (medical costs) — สัดส่วนนี้วัดเป็น Health Benefits Ratio (HBR/MLR), เป้าหมาย ~87–90% ของเบี้ยที่ได้รับ
- กำไรมาจากส่วนต่างระหว่างเบี้ยที่ได้รับ กับ (ค่ารักษา + ค่าบริหารจัดการ ~8-9%) — เป็นธุรกิจ margin บางมาก แต่ volume สูงมาก (revenue $180B+ TTM)
- ธุรกิจแบ่ง 3 ส่วนหลัก: **Medicaid** (ใหญ่สุด, ~60% ของ membership), **Medicare** (เล็กสุด, 980K MA members), **Marketplace/ACA** (3.5M members, กำลังหดตัวจาก PTC cliff)
- รายได้เสริมเล็กน้อยจาก specialty services (PBM ผ่าน Envolve, behavioral health, dental/vision) cross-sell ให้สมาชิกเดิม

## Emma's Conclusion

**Recommendation: AVOID at current price ($68.02).** Not a BUY — Value bucket gate requires MOS ≥15%; CNC's MOS is **-30.2%** by Emma's normalized P/B + P/E blended fair value of **$47.50**. Most of the apparent "discount" is an artifact of a one-time item, not a genuine valuation gap.

**Entry zone for future BUY consideration:** Price would need to fall to ≈$41.30 (a further ~39% decline) to reach MOS ≥15% on this FV, or the fair value itself would need to rise meaningfully (e.g., clean resolution of the PTC cliff + confirmed multi-quarter HBR stability without further actuarial surprises, which could justify a higher sustainable-g assumption). Given regime is TRANSITIONAL-CAUTIOUS (Force Deploy suspended per Atlas) this is moot for deployment purposes today regardless of MOS — but the negative MOS means CNC should **not** be added to `portfolio/buy_list.md` at this valuation; it belongs on the general watchlist for monitoring only (next FV re-verify trigger: Q3 2026 earnings, or confirmed ACA PTC legislative resolution).

**Conviction: 7/10** on the "not a BUY at $68.02" conclusion — reasonably confident because two independent valuation methods (Justified P/B, normalized P/E) plus a reverse-growth sanity check all converge on overvaluation. Tempered from higher because: (1) meaningful third-party data dispersion remains on ROIC/EV-EBITDA that Emma's normalization only partially resolves, (2) customer-concentration data used is 5-6 years stale, (3) the 2026 operational recovery (HBR 93.0%→89.6% YoY) is genuinely encouraging and could continue improving beyond what's modeled if no further actuarial miss occurs.
