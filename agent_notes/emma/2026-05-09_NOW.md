   - Historical: churn rate < 2% for customers > $1M ACV

2. **Network Effects (Secondary — Medium-High)**
   - 7,700+ technology partner integrations → more integrations = more valuable
   - AI Now Platform: data from 8,500+ customers improves AI models → data flywheel
   - Partner ecosystem: SI partners (Accenture, Deloitte, IBM) = distribution moat

3. **Intangible Assets (High)**
   - Brand: "The Enterprise Workflow Standard" — IT buyers default to NOW consideration
   - AI/ML IP: proprietary models trained on enterprise workflow data
   - CFA Level certification: enterprise IT professionals certified in NOW admin = human capital lock-in

4. **Data Advantage (High)**
   - Workflow data from 8,500 enterprise customers → richest enterprise process dataset globally
   - AI models trained on this data = better outcomes → reinforces switching costs

5. **Scale (Medium)**
   - R&D scale: $2B+/year enables AI investment beyond startup reach
   - Customer success scale: 1,000+ employees dedicated to customer expansion
