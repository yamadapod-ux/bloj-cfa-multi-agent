## 2. Business Model — Three Engines [CFA L2: Industry Analysis — Business Models]

### a) Electron — Small Launch (Operational, Mature)
- 50+ successful launches since 2017
- Price ~$8M, payload up to 320 kg
- Market: smallsat / dedicated ride-share / responsive launch
- **Issue:** small TAM (~$1B globally), commoditizing vs ride-share on F9
- Competitive pricing pressure but high cadence advantage
- ~12-15 launches/yr in steady state — profitability ~ break-even contribution

### b) Neutron — Medium-Lift (Pre-Operational — Maiden 2026 H2 target)
- Reusable, 13,000 kg payload (vs F9 ~17,000 kg)
- Target price: $50–55M per launch (vs F9 ~$67M list, ~$50M actual to repeat customers)
- Use case: constellation deployment, NSSL Phase 3 Lane 1, cargo to LEO
- **THIS IS THE THESIS** — binary outcome event
- Capex through 2026: ~$300M cumulative
- Maiden launch slipped from 2024 → 2025 → 2026 H2 (red flag #1)

### c) Space Systems — Satellite Components & Photon Platform
- Acquired Sinclair Interplanetary, ASI, SolAero, Mynaric
- Vertical: reaction wheels, star trackers, solar panels, separation systems, radios
- Photon: end-to-end satellite bus platform (ESCAPADE Mars mission, NASA contracts)
- Highest margin segment (30%+ GM, scalable)
- ~65% of TTM revenue, growing 35% YoY
