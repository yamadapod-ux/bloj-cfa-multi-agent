## Moat Analysis — Emma's Deep Dive

### Switching Cost Analysis (Wide — Emma's Key Moat):
**Why Custom ASIC = Deep Moat:**
1. NRE (Non-Recurring Engineering) contracts: Amazon signs 3-5 year NRE for each Trainium/Inferentia generation
2. MRVL IP (SerDes, PHY) embedded in every generation — to switch supplier = redesign from scratch
3. Silicon designers at Amazon/Google co-develop with MRVL teams → institutional knowledge shared → exit costs
4. Lead time: Custom ASIC development 24-36 months → switching = 2-3 product generations lost
5. **Emma's estimate:** Switching cost to replace MRVL in a single customer relationship = $200-500M direct cost + 2-3 years development time

### Intangible Assets (Wide):
- SerDes IP: MRVL owns patents on high-speed interconnect (56Gbps, 112Gbps, 224Gbps) — foundational AI cluster tech
- Coherent DSP: #1-2 market position in optical networking DSP chips (telecom + data center)
- Ethernet ASIC: Teralynx family — strong position in AI cluster switching
- Customer co-development relationships: engineers from Amazon/Google regularly at MRVL — knowledge moat

### Scale (Moderate):
- MRVL #2 in data center Ethernet switching after AVGO (Tomahawk family)
- MRVL #1 in coherent DSP for optical (ahead of Intel Silicon Photonics, Ciena)
- Scale advantage in SerDes: MRVL designs used in TSMC's most advanced nodes

## Emma's Investment Thesis — Why BUY MRVL at $72:

1. **"Switzerland" positioning is genuinely valuable** — no GPU maker can displace MRVL because MRVL serves the underlying infrastructure, not the compute layer
2. **Revenue de-risked by multiple hyperscaler relationships** — Amazon, Google, Microsoft Azure, Meta all use some MRVL components
3. **FY27E P/E of 17x** = if EPS consensus is right, MRVL is one of cheapest AI infrastructure names available
4. **Coherent optical + Ethernet = "boring" but structural** — every AI cluster needs this, not going away
5. **Matt Murphy track record deserves premium** — CEO who turned MRVL from commodity semi into AI infrastructure story

- Thesis clear and differentiated from NVDA/AMD/AVGO

- Main concern: client concentration at Amazon/Google — if one walks, revenue cliff
