|-------------|----------|-----------|---------|
| FDA 21 CFR 11 Compliance Lock-in | VERY HIGH | HIGH | $50-200M switching cost per enterprise; re-validation = 18-36 months |
| Data Network Effects | HIGH | MEDIUM-HIGH | 825K+ HCP connections; pharma data pooling |

**ESG-Adjusted FV: $196 × 0.955 = $187/share**

### Note on PBC Structure
Veeva Systems converted to Public Benefit Corporation in 2021. This is unusual for a public company. Peter Gassner has stated the mission ("helping life sciences companies bring products to market faster") takes priority. Historically this has been shareholder-aligned, but it creates governance optionality risk in M&A scenarios.
