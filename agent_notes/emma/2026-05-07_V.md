## Economic Moat Analysis

**Moat Rating: WIDE** *[CFA L2: Competitive Advantage — Porter's 5 Forces]*

| Moat Source | Strength | Durability | Notes |
|------------|---------|-----------|-------|
| Network Effects | Very High | 10-15 yr | 4.3B+ cards × 130M+ merchants × 200+ countries |
| Switching Costs | High | 10+ yr | Multi-year bank contracts, POS embedded systems, behavioral lock-in |
| Intangible Assets | High | Long-term | Brand top-10 global; banking licenses 200+ countries |
| Scale Economies | Very High | 15+ yr | $15T+ annual volume; fixed infra amortized over massive base |
