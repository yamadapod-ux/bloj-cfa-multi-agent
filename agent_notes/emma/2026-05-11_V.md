## Section 1: DCF FCFF Model [CFA L2: Equity Valuation — FCFF Model]

### DCF Assumptions

**Revenue Growth (Base Case):**
- FY2025A: $36.4B (+9.5% YoY) [Source: Atlas Data Package / Visa Annual Report est.]
- FY2026E: $40.0B (+9.9%) — consumer spending intact, cross-border recovery
- FY2027E: $43.8B (+9.5%) — EM expansion continues
- FY2028E: $47.4B (+8.2%) — slight deceleration
- FY2029E: $50.9B (+7.4%) — mature market effect
- FY2030E: $54.5B (+7.1%) — terminal period approach
- Terminal Growth Rate: 4.5% (nominal GDP + digital penetration premium)

**Revenue Growth (v1 vs v2 comparison):**
| Period | v1 Assumption | v2 Assumption | เหตุผลที่เปลี่ยน |
|--------|--------------|--------------|----------------|
| FY2026E | 9.0% | 9.9% | Q1 FY2026 beat ~10.2% YoY |
| Terminal | 4.0% | 4.5% | Digital payment secular growth ยังแข็งแกร่ง |
| WACC | 8.5% | 8.5% | Maintain (10Y treasury ไม่เปลี่ยน) |

**WACC Calculation:**
- Risk-free Rate: 4.25% [Source: Atlas Data Package / US Treasury 10Y est. 2026-05-11]
- Beta: 0.88 [Source: Atlas Data Package / Yahoo Finance est.]
- Equity Risk Premium: 5.0% [Source: Damodaran 2026 ERP estimate]
- Cost of Equity: 4.25% + 0.88 × 5.0% = **8.65%**
- Cost of Debt (after-tax): ~2.5% (effective rate ~3.5% × (1-28%))
- Debt/Total Capital: ~35%
- Equity/Total Capital: ~65%
- **WACC = 8.65% × 65% + 2.5% × 35% = 5.62% + 0.875% = 8.50%** (Maintain v1)
- *(assumption ของทีม — WACC 8.5% สะท้อน investment-grade debt cost + equity risk premium ปัจจุบัน)*

**Operating Assumptions:**
- Operating Margin: 66-68% (Visa maintains high margins due to scale)
- Tax Rate: 19.5% (effective — asset-light, no major inventory/COGS)
- D&A: ~$1.0B/year
- CapEx: ~$1.0B/year (asset-light model — network maintenance)
- Change in NWC: Minimal (~$0.3B/year) — payment network collects quickly

### FCFF Projections

| Year | Revenue ($B) | EBIT Margin | EBIT ($B) | EBIT(1-t) | D&A | CapEx | ΔNWC | FCFF ($B) |
|------|-------------|------------|----------|-----------|-----|-------|------|----------|
| FY2026E | $40.0 | 67% | $26.8 | $21.6 | $1.0 | $1.0 | $0.3 | **$21.3** |
| FY2027E | $43.8 | 67.5% | $29.6 | $23.8 | $1.0 | $1.0 | $0.3 | **$23.5** |
| FY2028E | $47.4 | 68% | $32.2 | $25.9 | $1.0 | $1.1 | $0.3 | **$25.5** |
| FY2029E | $50.9 | 68.5% | $34.9 | $28.1 | $1.0 | $1.1 | $0.3 | **$27.7** |
| FY2030E | $54.5 | 69% | $37.6 | $30.2 | $1.0 | $1.2 | $0.3 | **$29.7** |

**Terminal Value:**
- Terminal FCFF = $29.7B × (1 + 4.5%) = $31.0B
- Terminal Value = $31.0B / (8.5% - 4.5%) = **$775B**

**Present Values:**
- PV of FCFFs (FY2026-FY2030) = $21.3/1.085 + $23.5/1.085² + ... ≈ **$92B**
- PV of Terminal Value = $775B / 1.085⁵ ≈ **$515B**
- Enterprise Value = $92B + $515B = **$607B**
- Net Debt = $3.9B [Source: Atlas Data Package]
- Equity Value = $607B - $3.9B = **$603B**
- Shares Outstanding = 2.04B [Source: Atlas Data Package]
- **DCF Fair Value = $603B / 2.04B = $296/share**

*Note: เพิ่มจาก v1 DCF $294 → $296 (terminal growth rate เพิ่ม 0.5pp ช่วยได้เล็กน้อย)*

### Scenario Analysis — DCF with DOJ

**Bull Case (DOJ ถอน/แพ้ — 35% probability):**
- Revenue CAGR: 11% (5Y) — ไม่มี antitrust drag
- WACC: 8.0% (risk premium ลดหลัง DOJ resolved)
- Terminal Growth: 5.0%
- **DCF Bull FV = ~$360-380/share**

**Base Case (Settlement behavioral — 40% probability):**
- Revenue CAGR: 9.5% — debit revenue -5% adj.
- WACC: 8.5%
- Terminal Growth: 4.5%
- **DCF Base FV = ~$296/share**

**Bear Case (DOJ structural remedy — 25% probability):**
- Revenue CAGR: 6.0% (debit revenue capped)
- Structural debit revenue -15-20%: -$5-6B annual revenue impact
- WACC: 9.5% (risk premium + regulatory premium)
- Terminal Growth: 3.5%
- **DCF Bear FV = ~$180-200/share**

**Probability-Weighted DCF:**
- P-W FV = 35% × $370 + 40% × $296 + 25% × $190
- P-W FV = $129.5 + $118.4 + $47.5 = **$295/share** *(อิง Emma only — ยัง baseline; Quinn/Bear จะปรับ weights)*

2. **SME Interchange Impact — Social Risk** — Interchange fees กระทบ SME margins → ความเสี่ยง regulatory action ในหลายประเทศ → valuation impact: **-2% to -3%**
3. **Financial Exclusion Risk — Social** — คนจนที่ไม่มี bank account ยังเข้าถึง Visa ได้ยาก → ESG fund อาจ penalize → valuation impact: **-1%**

**ESG-Adjusted FV (v2):**

- ESG discount: -5% (governance DOJ + social)
- **ESG-Adj FV = $295 × 0.95 = ~$280/share**

*(v1 ESG-adj FV was $261 → v2 เพิ่มขึ้นเพราะ DOJ probability structural remedy ลดจาก 35% → 25%)*

## Section 4: Economic Moat Re-Assessment [CFA L2: Competitive Advantage — Economic Moat]

**Moat Rating: WIDE (ยังคง — แต่ durability risk เพิ่มขึ้น)**

| Moat Source | v1 Rating | v2 Rating | เหตุผล |
|------------|---------|---------|-------|
| Network Effects | Very High | Very High | ยังคงเป็น self-reinforcing; 4.4B cards ทำให้ใหม่เข้ามาแทบไม่ได้ |
| Switching Costs | High | High | Bank/merchant contracts multi-year; costly to switch |
| Intangible Assets | High | Medium-High | Brand แข็งแกร่งแต่ Apple Pay/Google Pay intermediation ลดทอน |
| Scale Economies | Very High | Very High | $15.8T volume = cost per transaction ต่ำมาก |
| Toll Road Model | Very High | High | DOJ challenge = legal risk ต่อ toll road structure |

**DOJ Impact ต่อ Moat:**
- ถ้า DOJ ชนะ structural remedy → บังคับ route debit ผ่าน FedNow/PIN networks = network effect debit ลดลง
- แต่ credit card network (ไม่ถูกฟ้อง) ยังคงเป็น Wide Moat อย่างเต็มที่
- Worst case: Debit moat narrow; Credit moat remains Wide
- **Emma's Assessment: Moat ยัง Wide แต่ "narrowing risk" เพิ่มจาก "uncertain" (v1) เป็น "elevated" (v2)**
