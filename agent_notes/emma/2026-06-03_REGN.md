## 1. BUSINESS DEEP DIVE [CFA L2: Industry Analysis — Porter's Five Forces]

### How Does Regeneron Make Money?

1. **Dupixent Collaboration Revenue** (~65–70% of total) — Regeneron ร่วมกับ Sanofi พัฒนา Dupixent (dupilumab) ซึ่งเป็น biologic ที่บล็อก IL-4/IL-13 pathway รักษาโรค inflammatory diseases รวมถึง Atopic Dermatitis, Asthma, COPD, Eosinophilic Esophagitis ฯลฯ REGN ได้รับ ~50% ของ Dupixent global net profit หลัง deducting Sanofi's commercialization expenses

2. **Eylea Revenue** (~15–20%) — Aflibercept ยาฉีดตา (VEGF inhibitor) รักษา wet AMD, diabetic macular edema ราคา decline จาก biosimilar competition แต่ Eylea HD (aflibercept 8mg) partially offsetting

3. **Other Products** (~10–15%) — Kevzara (IL-6R inhibitor RA), Praluent (PCSK9 inhibitor — ร่วมกับ Sanofi), Inmazeb (Ebola), Pozelimab (rare CHAPLE disease), Dupixent milestone payments

4. **Technology Licensing + Milestones** (~5%) — Regeneron มี proprietary VelociGene/VelocImmune platforms ซึ่งสร้าง antibody รวดเร็ว — ให้ licensing revenue + milestone payments จาก partners

5. **R&D Reimbursement from Sanofi** — Sanofi จ่าย portion ของ REGN R&D expenses สำหรับ collaboration programs (เพิ่มเติมจาก product profit share)

### Porter's Five Forces Analysis

| Force | ระดับ | เหตุผล |
|-------|-------|--------|
| **Threat of New Entrants** | LOW | FDA approval กว่า 10 ปี + $2-3B development cost + biologic manufacturing complexity = extremely high barrier |
| **Supplier Power** | LOW-MED | สัตว์ทดลอง/CRO = moderate; Active ingredient manufacturing in-house reduces supplier risk |
| **Buyer Power** | MED-HIGH | PBMs (CVS, Express Scripts, OptumRx) มี pricing power, insurance formulary negotiation; IRA Medicare negotiation emerging threat |
| **Threat of Substitutes** | MED | Other biologics in same indication (AstraZeneca Fasenra, GSK Nucala for asthma) แต่ Dupixent's breadth of indications reduces substitution risk significantly |
| **Competitive Rivalry** | MED | AbbVie/Sanofi/Pfizer/AstraZeneca compete ใน biologics แต่ mechanism differentiation limits head-to-head competition; Dupixent's IL-4/IL-13 dual blockade = unique target |

**Overall: LOW-MEDIUM competitive threat** — Barriers to entry very high; buyer power increasing via IRA but gradual

## 2. WIDE MOAT ANALYSIS [CFA L2: Economic Moat Analysis]

**Moat Rating: WIDE ✅**

### Moat Sources (5 dimensions):

| Source | Strength | Evidence |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **Intangible Asset (Patents)** | HIGH | Dupixent patent protection ~2031-2036 (no biosimilar threat for 5-10 years); 30+ patents covering different aspects |
| **R&D Platform (VelociGene/VelocImmune)** | HIGH | Proprietary antibody discovery platform = structural advantage in speed of antibody generation; produces best-in-class candidates consistently |
| **Switching Cost (Physician/Patient loyalty)** | MED-HIGH | Biologics = long titration/monitoring period; patients who respond switch rarely; physician familiarity compounds over time |
| **Scale + Manufacturing** | MED | Large-scale biologic manufacturing = cost advantage; Dupixent global scale with Sanofi distribution |
| **Data Network Effect (clinical evidence)** | MED | 8 approved indications = vast clinical dataset = further label expansion easier; evidence base = competitive moat |

**Dupixent Competitive Position:**
- First dual IL-4/IL-13 biologic = mechanism pioneer
- 8 approved indications = "platform drug" not single-indication
- COPD approval (Sep 2024) = ~37M US COPD patients = massive TAM expansion
- No direct mechanistic competitor (AstraZeneca Fasenra = IL-5; Pfizer = different pathway)
- Pipeline indication expansion = runway extends

**Moat Durability:**
- Strong: No biosimilar threat until 2031+
- Moderate risk: IRA drug price negotiation (if Dupixent added to list)
- Watch: New mechanism biologics from competitors (e.g., JAK inhibitors as oral alternative)
