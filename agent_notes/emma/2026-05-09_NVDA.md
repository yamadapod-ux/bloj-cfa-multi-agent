## Moat Deep Dive

### CUDA Ecosystem Depth (v2 Update)
- Active developers: 5M+ (ขึ้นจาก 4M = +25%)
- CUDA-enabled models ใน HuggingFace: 94% ของ top-1000 models
- PyTorch CUDA default: ยังเป็น standard — ROCm backend requires manual config
- ML frameworks native CUDA: PyTorch, TensorFlow, JAX, MXNet, Caffe2
- Switching cost estimate: $2-5M per enterprise team (retraining + workflow migration)
- **Emma Assessment: CUDA moat ยิ่งแข็งขึ้น ไม่อ่อนลง ใน 15 เดือนที่ผ่านมา**

### New Moat Vector: NIM (NVIDIA Inference Microservices)
- NIM = containerized optimized AI models ที่ run บน NVDA hardware
- Business model: ซื้อ GPU แล้ว run NIM → software upsell layer
- Switching cost layer เพิ่ม: ถ้า enterprise ใช้ NIM + NVDA hardware พร้อมกัน switching cost ทวีคูณ
- Comparable: Apple App Store effect — platform lock-in via software on hardware

## Peer Comparison Methodology

Peers selected: AMD (direct GPU competitor), Intel (CPU/GPU adjacent), AVGO (fabless semi/AI ASIC), TSMC (foundry — upstream risk)

**Why TSMC as peer?**
- TSMC = upstream risk / moat dependency (NVDA 100% TSMC for advanced nodes)
- Taiwan geopolitical risk affects NVDA supply chain directly
- Comparing EV/Sales and P/E helps understand market pricing of foundry vs fabless premium

**Relative Valuation Conclusion:**
- NVDA PEG 0.55x vs peer median 0.85x = 35% discount on growth-adjusted basis
- NVDA ROIC 83% vs median 17% = 4.9x premium quality
- **Verdict:** Premium valuation P/E (+23%) is MORE than justified by quality premium (ROIC 4.9x) and growth premium (5x median) — P/E discount vs quality/growth is actually cheap
