## Moat Analysis Detail

### Switching Costs (Moderate):
- x86 instruction set = AMD CPU fully compatible → low switching cost FROM Intel (AMD wins)
- But CUDA vs ROCm = high switching cost FOR GPU customers switching FROM NVDA to AMD
- Emma believes ROCm switching cost will decrease over 3-5 years as software matures

### Intangible Assets (Wide):
- Zen architecture represents 10+ years of microarchitecture R&D — difficult to replicate
- CDNA (Compute) and RDNA (Graphics) architecture families — distinct IP moats
- 3,000+ patents in CPU/GPU design (estimate)
- AMD brand = "quality Intel alternative" strongly established

### Scale (Moderate):
- Fabless model: TSMC N5/N3/N2 access — but NVDA and Apple also compete for same nodes
- AMD spending ~$3B/yr on R&D (7.5% of revenue vs NVDA 10%) — adequate but not dominant
- Lisa Su strategy: focus on high-performance computing, not chase consumer volume

### Network Effects (Narrow → growing):
- ROCm ecosystem: ~500K developers vs CUDA's 4M+ — significant gap
- But the gap is closing: GitHub activity on ROCm repos +80% YoY (2024-2025)
- Enterprise ISVs (SAP, Oracle) beginning to certify AMD GPU support

**Emma's Moat Conclusion:** NARROW today, trajectory toward MODERATE in 3 years if ROCm adoption continues. If ROCm hits 1M developers + major ISV certification wave → re-rate to WIDE possible in 5 years.
