## 2. Economic Moat Analysis [CFA L2: Equity — Economic Moat, Porter's 5 Forces]

**Moat Rating: WIDE** (Emma conviction: 9/10 on moat durability)

### Five Sources of Moat:

**1. Switching Cost (PRIMARY — strongest source)**
- FDA 21 CFR Part 11 compliance: Veeva Vault is validated under FDA computer system validation (CSV) requirements
- Switching a validated GxP system requires: 18-24 months re-validation, FDA notification, risk of audit failure during transition
- Average customer rips-and-replaces Veeva: virtually NEVER (retention rate ~99%)
- This is the strongest switching cost Emma has seen across all portfolio names — stronger than Adobe Creative Cloud, stronger than CUDA

**2. Intangible Assets (regulatory knowledge)**
- 20+ years of life sciences-specific workflow IP embedded in product
- FDA, EMA, PMDA regulatory frameworks built-in natively
- "Life Sciences DNA" cannot be replicated by horizontal SaaS (Salesforce, SAP) without 5-10 year rebuild

**3. Network Effects (Data Cloud)**
- Veeva Data Cloud (formerly Veeva OpenData) aggregates data from 400K+ HCPs across 60+ countries
- Each new pharma customer adds data density → improves analytics for all customers
- Network effects strengthen with scale — similar to Visa's payment network but in healthcare data

**4. Scale Advantages**
- ~80% penetration of global top-50 pharma companies (by revenue)
- 1,000+ customer pharma/biotech/CRO accounts
- Integration depth: average enterprise customer uses 5-7 Vault products (not just 1)

**5. Cost Advantage (Specialized Focus)**
- R&D concentrated entirely on life sciences → better product at lower unit R&D cost vs horizontal SaaS that must serve all industries

**Porter's Five Forces Assessment:**
- Rivalry: LOW-MODERATE (only Medidata/Veeva in clinical; IQVIA in data; no pure SaaS peer at scale)
- Supplier Power: LOW (cloud infrastructure AWS/Azure = commoditized)
- Buyer Power: LOW (switching costs prohibitive; buyers are fragmented pharma companies)
- Threat of New Entrants: VERY LOW (FDA validation moat + domain expertise = 5-10yr runway)
- Threat of Substitutes: LOW-MODERATE (Salesforce Life Sciences Cloud = partial substitute for Commercial Cloud ONLY; not Vault)
