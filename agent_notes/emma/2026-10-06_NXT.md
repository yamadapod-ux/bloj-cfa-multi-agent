## 🏢 Business Deep Dive (REQUIRED business description — CIO standard for all future reports)

**What NXT actually does:** Nextracker designs, manufactures, and sells solar tracker systems — motorized structures (its NX Horizon product line) that tilt utility-scale solar panels to follow the sun across the day, increasing energy yield versus fixed-tilt installations — paired with its proprietary **TrueCapture** software that further optimizes tracker positioning in real time using weather/shading data. Through 2026 acquisitions (**Apex Power** for inverters, **Prevalon** for battery energy storage systems, pending **Zimmermann PV-Steel Group** for European steel/racking manufacturing) it is expanding from a pure tracker-hardware company into a broader power-conversion/storage platform. **Its customers are utility-scale solar project developers, EPC (engineering-procurement-construction) contractors, and independent power producers building large solar farms — increasingly including hyperscalers and data-center operators** sourcing power infrastructure to meet AI-driven electricity demand (91% of new U.S. generation capacity added in H1 2026 was solar+storage, Nextpower's own Q1 FY27 shareholder letter). **This is NOT a residential-solar company** — it has no exposure to the Section 25D residential ITC expiration that structurally impaired RUN this round.
