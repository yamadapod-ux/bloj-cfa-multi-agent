## 1. Business Overview [CFA L2: Industry Analysis — Business Model]

Fair Isaac Corporation (FICO) operates two segments:

**Scores Segment (~70% revenue):** Provides FICO credit scores to lenders for consumer lending decisions. FICO Score is embedded in GSE (Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac) underwriting standards, creating near-mandatory use in mortgage origination. Pricing power has compounded — from ~$0.50/score (2013) to ~$3.50/score (2024). *Source: Company 10-K FY2025 (Atlas estimate); industry reports*

**Software Segment (~30% revenue):** FICO Platform — cloud-based decision management SaaS. Growing faster (Software +11.9% YoY Q1 FY2026 vs Scores +6.4%). Transitioning from legacy license → recurring cloud ARR. *Source: Atlas Data Package Q1 FY2026 estimates*

**Asset-Light Model:** Operating margin ~47–49%, ROIC ~68%, FCF margin ~39%. Capital requirements minimal — primarily R&D + sales. Classic franchise economics.

## 9. Investment Thesis

**Core Thesis:** FICO is a best-in-class financial analytics franchise (ROIC 68%, gross margin 86%, near-monopoly in credit scoring) that has been repriced -49% from ATH due to regulatory overhang (DOJ + FHFA). The market is pricing ~35–40% permanent impairment to FICO's business model. Emma's base case (45% probability) = DOJ results in price cap/consent decree (NOT structural remedy) = ~-10–15% revenue impact = still justifies ~$1,300+ FV. Current price $1,088 provides margin of safety in all scenarios except structural remedy (~5% probability).

**Bull Case:** DOJ settles with mild price cap + rates fall → mortgage volumes recover → software accelerates → $1,900+

**Bear Case:** DOJ price cap + FHFA dual-scoring fully implemented → $720

**Extreme:** Structural remedy forces score model divestiture → $350
