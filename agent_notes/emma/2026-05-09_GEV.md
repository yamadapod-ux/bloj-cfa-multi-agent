## Full Calculations

### 1. DCF Valuation [CFA L2: Equity Valuation — FCFF Model]

**WACC Calculation:**
- Risk-free rate: 4.30% [Source: US Treasury 10Y ณ 2026-05-09]
- Equity Risk Premium (ERP): 4.5% [Source: Damodaran Jan 2026 estimate — US market]
- Beta: ~1.10 (GEV ≤2Y history — ใช้ comparable industrial beta; GEV IPO Apr 2024) [Source: *(assumption ของทีม — GEV track record สั้น ใช้ peer industrial beta median: PWR 1.15, ETN 1.05, EMR 1.00 → median 1.05, ปรับ GEV เล็กน้อยขึ้น 1.10 เพราะ wind segment risk premium)*]
- Cost of Equity = 4.30% + 1.10 × 4.50% = **9.25%**
- Debt: ~$8.5B [Source: GEV 10-K FY2024]
- Total Capital: ~$8.5B + $119B = ~$127.5B (market cap basis)
- D/C ratio: ~6.7%
- Cost of Debt: ~5.0% (pre-tax), After-tax: 5.0% × (1-21%) = 3.95%
- **WACC = 9.25% × 93.3% + 3.95% × 6.7% = 8.63% + 0.26% = ~8.9%** *(ปัดเป็น 9.0% ใช้คำนวณ)*

**FCFF Historical Base (FY2024):**
- EBIT: ~$2.0B (adj.)
- Tax rate: 21%
- EBIT(1-t): ~$1.58B
- D&A: ~$900M
- ΔNWC: ~($200M) — working capital ปรับตัวหลัง spin-off
- Capex: ~($700M)
- **FCFF FY2024 = $1.58B + $0.9B - $0.2B - $0.7B = ~$1.58B** [Source: GEV 10-K FY2024 — calculated]

**DCF 2-Stage Assumptions:**

**Terminal Value (2030 FCFF basis):**

## Economic Moat Analysis [CFA L2: Equity — Economic Moat]

**Moat Level: NARROW (approaching Wide in Gas Power + Grid; Wide offset by Wind drag)**

| แหล่งที่มา | GEV | ระดับ | เหตุผล |
|-----------|-----|------|--------|
| Switching Costs | High | ✅ Strong | Gas turbine service lifecycle 30-40 ปี; ลูกค้าต้องใช้ GEV parts + service → ต้นทุนเปลี่ยนสูงมาก |
| Intangible Assets | High | ✅ Strong | 100+ ปี IP จาก GE; HA-class gas turbine คือ most efficient ในโลก; 70%+ global installed base of large-frame turbines |
| Scale | Moderate | ✅ Moderate | Service network global + manufacturing scale หน่วยใหม่ cost competitive |
| Network Effects | Low | ❌ Weak | ไม่ใช่ network effect business; service relationship ≠ network |
| Cost Advantages | Moderate | ✅ Moderate | Installed base ≥$95B service backlog = recurring revenue moat; cheaper per unit than greenfield |

**Moat Durability:** Gas Power & Grid = Wide (20+ ปี backlog lock-in); Wind = None (structurally unprofitable, limited IP advantage vs Vestas/Siemens Gamesa)

## Investment Thesis

**Why GEV is compelling:**
1. **AI Data Center Power Demand = Direct Beneficiary** — hyperscalers ต้องการ reliable baseload power; gas turbines คือ fastest-to-deploy + most reliable → GEV Gas Power backlog กำลังเร่งตัว (Q4 2024: $5B+ orders/quarter)
2. **Grid Modernization Supercycle** — US grid aging 40+ ปี ต้องการ upgrade massive; IRA + IIJA fund ~$65B for grid; GEV Electrification segment โตเร็วสุด >20% YoY
3. **Service Backlog = Recurring Revenue Machine** — $95B+ Gas Power service backlog = 30+ ปีของ recurring revenue ที่มี predictability สูง; customer cannot easily switch
4. **Wind Drag Clearing** — offshore wind fixed-price contracts ส่วนใหญ่จะ complete 2026-2027; FY2027+ EBITDA margin expansion significant
5. **Spin-off Track Record Short = Opportunity** — ตลาดยังไม่ fully-price standalone GEV; ถ้า Wind drag resolves → re-rating significant

**Why to be cautious:**
1. Wind segment ยังขาดทุน — timeline อาจยาวกว่าคาด
2. Premium P/E ต้องการ execution flawless
3. Spin-off ประวัติสั้น — management track record independent ยังสั้น
