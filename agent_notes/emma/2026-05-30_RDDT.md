## 1. Business Deep Dive [CFA L2: Industry Analysis — Porter's Five Forces]

### How Does Reddit Make Money?
1. **Advertising (94% of revenue):** Display + video ads served to 126.8M Daily Active Unique (DAUq) users across 100,000+ niche communities. ARPU $5.23 Q1 2026 (+9% QoQ) — growing via performance ads mix shift.
2. **AI Data Licensing (~6% of revenue, high-growth):** Licensing authentic human-generated UGC conversation data to AI companies. Google deal: $60M/year (renewal 2027). OpenAI deal: active. Total AI licensing potential $400M/year by 2027.
3. **Reddit Pro + Business Tools (nascent):** Premium advertising tools, self-serve ad platform. Currently bundled in ad revenue.

### Porter's Five Forces
| Force | Rating | Rationale |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| **Competitive Rivalry** | HIGH | Meta "Forum" (May 2026), Discord (niche communities), X/Twitter, Quora, specialized forums |
| **Threat of New Entrants** | MEDIUM | Network effects create flywheel moat; but Meta has 3B MAU distribution as massive entry advantage |
| **Bargaining Power — Suppliers** | LOW | Suppliers = users who generate content. Reddit has platform control; moderators are volunteers |
| **Bargaining Power — Buyers** | MEDIUM-HIGH | Advertisers have many social platforms to choose from; Google/AI data deal customers are concentrated |
| **Threat of Substitutes** | HIGH | TikTok for entertainment, Instagram/X for social, ChatGPT for information retrieval — Reddit's info-seeking use case is under pressure from AI |

### Market Share Trend (3-Year)
- Reddit DAUq: 2023 ~73M → 2024 ~104M → Q1 2026 126.8M (+17% YoY) — gaining share
- Social media user base growing: Reddit expanding vs stagnant Twitter/X, niche vs Meta

### Customer Concentration
- **Ad Revenue:** No single advertiser >10% of revenue (diversified by design) — LOW concentration risk

## 2. Economic Moat Analysis [CFA L2: Economic Moat Framework]

### Moat Sources
1. **Network Effects (STRONG):** Reddit's 100,000+ niche subreddit communities are self-reinforcing. Users post where communities exist; communities form where users are. Migration cost = lose community context + karma + relationships. "The Nth user makes the (N-1)th user's experience better" in specialized communities.

2. **Switching Costs (MODERATE):** Reddit karma system, posting history, community moderation roles. Power users (Mods with 5+ years) have significant sunk cost. However, regular lurkers have low switching cost.

3. **Unique Asset — UGC Data (EMERGING WIDE MOAT):** Reddit's 18+ years of authentic human conversation is irreplicable. AI models need training data that sounds human; Reddit provides this at scale across every conceivable topic. This data moat becomes MORE valuable as AI training demand grows. No new platform can replicate 18 years of organic conversation.

4. **Intangible Asset — Brand:** "The front page of the internet" — unique cultural positioning as authentic vs. curated social media.

### Meta "Forum" Threat Assessment
Meta launched "Forum" app in May 2026 — attempting to replicate Reddit's community discussion format.

**Why Reddit survives Meta "Forum":**
- Content quality: Reddit's UGC is ~18 years deep. Meta Forum starts from zero.
- Community trust: Reddit communities have self-governance culture; Meta = ad-driven distrust
- Search indexability: Reddit threads rank highly in Google Search. Meta Forum (Facebook) has historically poor Google indexability.
- Demographics: Reddit skews 18-35 male technical; Meta = broader but Reddit's demographic = highest ARPU for performance ads

**Why Meta "Forum" is a real risk:**
- Distribution: 3B+ MAU vs Reddit 100M — user acquisition cost nearly zero for Meta
- AI investment: Meta AI integration in Forum could accelerate content quality
- Advertiser defection: If Meta Forum reaches scale, advertisers may reallocate budget

**Verdict:** Meta "Forum" = MEDIUM-HIGH competitive risk, but Reddit's moat is defensible over 3-5 year horizon. The AI data licensing revenue stream is entirely unaffected by Meta competition.

### Moat Rating: NARROW-to-WIDE (transitioning)
- Advertising business: NARROW (competition real, ARPU gap vs META)
- AI Data business: WIDE (irreplaceable data asset, growing value)
- Combined: NARROW+ trending toward WIDE as AI licensing scales
