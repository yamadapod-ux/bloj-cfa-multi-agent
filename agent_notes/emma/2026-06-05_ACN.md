## 1. Business Deep Dive [CFA L2: Industry Analysis — Porter's Five Forces]

### How Does Accenture Make Money? (Plain Language)
1. **Strategy & Consulting** (~25% revenue): เป็นที่ปรึกษา C-suite เรื่อง business strategy, AI transformation, digital roadmap — fee ต่อชั่วโมง/project
2. **Technology Services** (~45% revenue): วาง IT systems, cloud migration, AI implementation — โปรเจกต์ขนาดใหญ่ multi-year
3. **Operations / Managed Services** (~30% revenue): รัน IT systems ให้ลูกค้าระยะยาว — recurring revenue, higher retention
4. **Industry X** (cross-cutting): Smart factories, digital supply chains, embedded software — premium margin segment
5. **AI Revenue Growing Fast**: AI bookings $3B+ ใน 12 เดือนล่าสุด = AI = growth driver ใหม่ที่ตลาดยังไม่เชื่อ

### Porter's Five Forces [CFA L2: Industry Analysis]

| Force | Level | เหตุผล |
|-------|-------|--------|
| Competitive Rivalry | HIGH | IBM, Infosys, TCS, Capgemini, Wipro, McKinsey/Bain (strategy) — market fragmented แต่ top-tier competitive |
| Threat of New Entrants | LOW | Scale + relationships + industry expertise = 10–20 year build time; Big Tech (Google/MSFT) bundling risk is real but different market |
| Bargaining Power of Buyers | MEDIUM | Enterprise buyers negotiation power, but switching cost (trained talent + institutional knowledge) high |
| Bargaining Power of Suppliers | MEDIUM-HIGH | AI talent shortage = wage inflation pressure; top consultants command premium |
| Threat of Substitutes | MEDIUM | AI tools (GitHub Copilot, enterprise AI) = partial substitute, but implementation gap remains large |

**Industry Assessment**: Favorable for incumbents. Wide Moat through scale + relationships + talent pool.
