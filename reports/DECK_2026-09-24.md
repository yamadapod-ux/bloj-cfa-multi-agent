## 💡 Investment Thesis

**Bull case:** Debt-free, $1.6B cash, $4.7B buyback authorization, 56.4% gross margin, ROIC 33.67% (very high quality business by capital-efficiency standards). Market has de-rated the stock to ~10.8x forward earnings — unusually cheap for this quality level, largely because of a legitimate re-rating of expected forward growth, not a technical anomaly (stock split ruled out — 6-for-1 split was in Sept 2024, well before current price series).

**Bear case (independent, from subagent Bear):** The Q1 FY27 "beat" masks real deterioration — operating income fell 6% while SG&A rose 12.7%; EPS "growth" came from a 7.4% share-count reduction (buybacks), not operating leverage. Full-year operating-margin guidance was cut from ~22.8% to ~21.5%. Analyst downgrades are real and recent (BMO Underperform $70 PT 9/8/2026; KGI, Raymond James, Zacks, Weiss all downgraded in 2026). Short interest rose +23.6% in the latest reporting period to 5.8% of float. HOKA's growth deceleration (30%+ → high-single-digit) may be structural (brand maturing past $3B, resurgent competition from Nike/On Running), not cyclical.

**Resolution:** Dual-gate technically PASSES (MOS +22.4% ≥15%, Conviction ~6 ≥6) but this is a **borderline pass, not a high-conviction slam dunk** — Bear's case is substantive, not noise.
