## 🏢 Business Deep Dive
*[CFA L2: Industry Analysis]*

**How does NXT make money?** Nextracker designs, manufactures, and sells solar tracker systems — motorized structures (its NX Horizon product line) that tilt utility-scale solar panels to follow the sun across the day, increasing energy yield versus fixed-tilt installations — paired with its proprietary **TrueCapture** software that further optimizes tracker positioning in real time using weather and shading data. Through 2026 acquisitions (Apex Power for inverters, Prevalon for battery energy storage, pending Zimmermann PV-Steel Group for European expansion) it is broadening from a pure tracker-hardware company into a wider power-conversion/storage platform. **Its customers are utility-scale solar project developers, EPC contractors, and independent power producers building large solar farms — increasingly including hyperscalers and data-center operators** sourcing power infrastructure for AI-driven electricity demand (91% of new U.S. generation capacity added in H1 2026 was solar+storage). **This is explicitly NOT a residential-solar company** — it carries none of the Section 25D ITC-expiration exposure that structurally impaired RUN this scout round.

**Porter's Five Forces:**
- **Threat of new entrants: LOW** — #1 global tracker market share for 11 consecutive years (55% U.S., 30% global, Wood Mackenzie 2025); scale, bankability track record, and proprietary software create real switching friction for EPC customers.
- **Bargaining power of suppliers: MEDIUM** — exposed to steel/component input costs and tariff-affected imported materials, partially offset by domestic-content manufacturing (IRA 45X qualifying).
- **Bargaining power of buyers: MEDIUM** — large utility-scale developers have negotiating leverage on price, but NXT's backlog (>$5.5B) and software differentiation reduce pure price-competition.
- **Threat of substitutes: LOW-MEDIUM** — fixed-tilt racking is a lower-cost substitute for trackers, but trackers' yield advantage (typically 15-25% more energy) makes them the default choice for most utility-scale projects in favorable climates.
- **Competitive rivalry: MEDIUM** — ARRY is the clearest direct competitor; FSLR/ENPH operate in adjacent but distinct sub-industries (module manufacturing, residential inverters respectively).

**Market Share Trend:** #1 global tracker share maintained for 11 consecutive years (stated management metric, Wood Mackenzie-sourced) — gaining, not losing, share; expanding global project footprint to 50+ countries.

**Customer Concentration:** Not separately disclosed in the sources reviewed this session — flagged as a data gap, not fabricated.

**Geography Revenue Breakdown:** Primarily U.S.-weighted (55% U.S. tracker share cited specifically vs. 30% global), with international expansion (Zimmermann acquisition targets Europe) underway — specific %/YoY geographic breakdown not independently sourced this session; flagged as a data gap rather than estimated.

**Sources:** stockanalysis.com/stocks/nxt/ (About section); Nextpower Q1 FY27 8-K and earnings call transcript (BigGo Finance, Jul 30 2026); `agent_notes/emma/2026-10-06_NXT.md`.
