Marvell Technology's custom silicon thesis has been validated beyond expectation. The company delivered $4.88B Data Center revenue in FY2026 (vs v1 estimate $3.5–4B), confirmed NRE renewals from Amazon and Google, and positioned itself as the primary beneficiary of hyperscaler ASIC buildout worth $25–30B in 2026 and $50–60B by 2028.

Only in the ultra-bull scenario (35% revenue CAGR + 40% terminal EBIT margin) does $171 represent fair value.

The appropriate action: TRIM the existing 3% position to 1.5%, raise the stop loss to $110 to protect substantial gains (+137%), and wait for either (a) a price pullback to $130–145 to re-add, or (b) new catalyst confirmation (4th hyperscaler, ROIC expansion to 20%+) that would justify initiating at higher prices.

The story is not over. The thesis is not broken. But at $171, you are paying tomorrow's price for today's position.
