## 🏢 Business Deep Dive [CFA L2: Industry & Company Analysis]
**How does SLM make money?**
- Lends to students for university and graduate school, mostly with a parent/relative as cosigner (84%), and earns the spread: loan yield 10.25% − cost of funds 4.13%.
- Sells part of the loan book to investors to book a gain immediately ($3.75B sold in 1H26 → gain of $161M).
- Funds itself with bank deposits (~$19.9B) and ABS.
- Returns most of its profit to shareholders: buybacks $561M TTM, dividend $0.52/share.
