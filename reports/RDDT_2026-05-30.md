## 💡 Investment Thesis [CFA L2: Equity Valuation — Growth Company Framework]

### Three-Layer Bull Case

**Layer 1 — Advertising Momentum (Priced In)**
Reddit's ad revenue grew +74% YoY in Q1 2026, driven by ARPU expansion from $5.23. Monetization gap vs META (~$40 ARPU) implies 5-8× potential ARPU growth over 5-10 years. Performance advertising (ROI-measurable) is growing faster than brand advertising on Reddit — this is structurally defensible against Meta Forum because Reddit's niche communities enable hyper-targeted ad placement unavailable elsewhere.

**Layer 2 — AI Data Licensing (Partially Priced In)**
Reddit holds 18+ years of authentic human conversation across every conceivable topic. This data is:
- **Irreplaceable:** Cannot be generated synthetically (AI models need human-written training data to sound human)
- **Scalable:** Zero marginal cost to license additional data; no physical infrastructure needed
- **Growing in value:** As AI training demand increases, Reddit's bargaining power increases each training cycle
- **Current:** Google $60M/yr + OpenAI (undisclosed). Potential: $400M/yr by 2027 per multiple analyst sources (Yahoo Finance: https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/reddit-ai-data-licensing-google-121250434.html)

At 12.6× EV/Revenue, the AI licensing moat is effectively being valued at the same multiple as the cyclical advertising business — this is a mispricing if licensing reaches $300-400M/yr.

**Layer 3 — International Monetization (Not Priced In)**
Reddit's International ARPU is materially below US ARPU. As performance advertising infrastructure matures globally, International ARPU convergence toward $3-5 (from current ~$1-2) represents incremental revenue growth of $400-600M/yr not in current models.

## 🏢 Business Deep Dive [CFA L2: Industry Analysis — Porter's Five Forces]

### How Does Reddit Make Money?
1. **Advertising (~94% of revenue):** Display + video ads across 100,000+ subreddits. Advertisers target by interest community, keywords, demographics. ARPU $5.23 Q1 2026, growing +45% YoY.
2. **AI Data Licensing (~6%, growing):** Licensing UGC conversation data corpus to AI companies (Google, OpenAI). Google deal: $60M/yr (renewal 2027). Total potential: $400M/yr by 2027.
3. **Premium Products (nascent):** Reddit Pro (business features), premium advertising tools — currently a rounding error.

### Porter's Five Forces
| Force | Rating | Rationale |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| **Competitive Rivalry** | HIGH | Meta "Forum" (May 2026), Discord, X/Twitter, Quora |
| **Threat of New Entrants** | MEDIUM | Network effects strong; but Meta entry changes calculus |
| **Bargaining Power — Suppliers** | LOW | Suppliers = volunteer content creators; platform controls distribution |
| **Bargaining Power — Buyers (Advertisers)** | MEDIUM-HIGH | Many platforms compete for ad budget; but Reddit niche targeting = differentiation |
| **Threat of Substitutes** | HIGH | AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity) substitutes Reddit's information-seeking use case |

### Market Share
Reddit DAUq: 73M (2023) → 104M (2024) → 126.8M Q1 2026 (+17% YoY) — GAINING share vs Twitter/X, Discord — Source: SEC 10-K + Q1 8-K

### Customer Concentration
- Advertising: No single advertiser >10% revenue ✅ LOW risk
- Data Licensing: Google = primary deal (~$60M/yr ~6% revenue) — WATCH if scales to $400M/yr (>14% revenue concentration)
- Traffic: Google referral ~25-30% of visits — HIGH dependency risk (Source: Atlas Data Package)

### Geography Revenue

- International: ~20-25%, growing faster
